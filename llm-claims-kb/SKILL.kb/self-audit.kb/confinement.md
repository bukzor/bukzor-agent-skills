# Confinement

A claim uses only the words its theory admits -- every `ontology:` on
the path up to the ledger, plus its priors', transitively. The scan is
a grep per theory for load-bearing nouns outside that list. A violation
is either a misplaced claim (move it to the outermost theory that
admits its words) or an understated ontology (widen it on purpose,
which is a revision to a signed claim and takes a fresh signature).
An undefined theory stipulates nothing, so its claims answer to the
path above it -- and the first claim there that needs a word of its
own is the bill for the defining claim nobody wrote.
Silence is not health: an ontology nobody has widened in a while may
just mean nobody scanned.
