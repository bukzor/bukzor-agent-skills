# Confinement

A claim uses only the words its theory admits -- its collection's
`ontology:` plus its priors', transitively. The scan is a grep per
theory for load-bearing nouns outside that list. A violation is
either a misplaced claim (move it to the earliest theory that admits
its words) or an understated ontology (widen it on purpose, in the
collection's CLAUDE.md). Silence is not health: an ontology nobody
has widened in a while may just mean nobody scanned.
