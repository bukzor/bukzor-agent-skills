# Confinement

An `ontology:` excludes: a word listed there is that theory's, and its
siblings may not use it. Descendants need no permission -- they read
every word their container coins -- and another ledger is out of
jurisdiction. So the scan is not "a word off the list"; it is "a
sibling saying an owned word", and the finding is one of three. The
word was never the owner's to take -- topic vocabulary rather than a
coinage, however central to the subject -- so cull it. Or the claim is
misfiled, and moves under the owner. Or the two genuinely mean
different things by it, and one of them uniquifies -- usually by
restoring the phrase its own prose already uses, since a list entry
truncated to its head noun is how an ownable term becomes unownable.
Silence is not health: an ontology nobody has narrowed in a while may
just mean nobody scanned.
