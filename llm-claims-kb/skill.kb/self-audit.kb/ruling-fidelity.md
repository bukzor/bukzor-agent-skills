# Ruling Fidelity

A claim citing a ruling carries the owner's authority, so a paraphrase at a
different scope signs the owner's name to something unsaid ([SCOPE_KEPT]).

For each claim you touched whose `authority:` or `[!@...]` callout quotes a
ruling, read the body against the quote clause by clause: every qualifier,
condition, and alternative in the quote survives. A finding is restored, or, if
the narrowing is the agent's proposal, the claim says so and its standing drops
to `agent` until the owner rules.
