# Every Claim Sound, Open, or Retracted

The one invariant. At any moment, for both parties, each claim is
believed sound, openly unsettled, or withdrawn — and never in the fourth
state, the common one, where nobody remembers which.

Everything else in the design is instrumental to holding it. A feature
that does not help is decoration; a feature that lets a claim occupy the
fourth state is a defect however convenient, because the fourth state is
not a gap in the record but a false record — the claim reads as settled
to anyone who does not remember otherwise.

The invariant is what survives across rungs of the ladder
(one-rung-of-a-ladder.md). A proof assistant enforces it; this notation
only records it; a chat with no notation at all loses it. That is the
whole difference the ledger makes.
