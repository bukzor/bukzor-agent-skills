# Bare Form Stays Legal

Every elaboration's all-defaults case is the prior level's syntax: a
bare `XY: text` line remains legal and meaningful in the fullest
system. Power features cost tokens only where used, and upgrades are
purely additive — existing content never needs migration, and users of
the smallest deployment (a preferences paragraph) interoperate with
users of the largest.
