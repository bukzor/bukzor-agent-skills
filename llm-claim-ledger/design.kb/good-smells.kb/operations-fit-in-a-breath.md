# Meta-Operations Fit in a Breath

Governing the ledger — adjudicating, contesting, revising — takes
one-line commands over labels: `claim accept: OP` is a complete
adjudication. Reference-by-label compresses discussion of claims to the
claims' names, so the cost of acting on the ledger stays below the cost
of ignoring it. If governance takes paragraphs, the ledger goes
ungoverned.
