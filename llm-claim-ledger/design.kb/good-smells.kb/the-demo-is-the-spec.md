# The Demo Is the Spec

The format exemplar carries the semantics: a reader who imitates the
demo behaves correctly without reading the policy. Self-demonstrating
examples (`LC: we Label our Claims`; a proposal line marked `?`) are
the strongest instruction channel per token, so demo lines are spent on
the behaviors most likely to be fumbled — and a policy clause with no
demo line should expect weak compliance.
