# The Demo Is the Spec

The format exemplar carries the semantics: a reader who imitates the
demo behaves correctly without reading the policy. That makes demo
lines the strongest instruction channel per token, so they are spent on
the behaviors most likely to be fumbled — a proposal left marked `?`, a
conclusion resting on a mixed premise set — and a policy clause with no
demo line should expect weak compliance.

Self-demonstrating is not self-describing. A demo whose claims are
*about* labels and sigils teaches the vocabulary but never shows the
notation carrying a subject, which is the only thing a reader will ever
do with it; and labels chosen to echo their own claim text model a
naming rule nobody should follow.
