# Enforceable Invariants Win

Between two rules expressing the same discipline, adopt the one a dumb
tool can check. A ledger's structural rules are unenforced by default,
and an unenforced rule decays in silence: "no premise arrow points
upward" stood unviolated on paper through a whole ledger's history while
19 claims sat rootless and a group captioned substrate-free carried
substrate vocabulary in every line. The same discipline restated as
vocabulary — "a claim uses only the words its group admits" — is one
`grep` away, and found violations in files edited the day it was written.
DM asks that the notation reward the dumbest consumer; this asks the same
of its rules.
