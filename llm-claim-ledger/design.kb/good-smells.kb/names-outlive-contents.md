# Names Outlive Contents

Labels name the locus of contention, not the current conclusion. A
claim can reverse polarity under revision and keep its name, so every
existing reference stays valid and revision is ergonomic: supersede in
place, same label, new content. A label that encodes the answer ("NC:
obligations Name their Check") dies with its first revision; a label
that names the question ("RR: route requirement") survives any answer.
