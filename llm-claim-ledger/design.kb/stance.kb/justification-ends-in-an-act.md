# Justification Ends in an Act

Every regress this design runs into terminates in an act, not in a
further truth: someone did something, and is named for it. Three
regresses run here — inference, design, and authority — and one pattern
stops all three.

The alternative is a foundation that is a truth: some proposition
self-evident enough that questioning stops there on its own merits. This
design declines it, not because such propositions cannot exist but
because in the settings a ledger serves there is never one to hand.
What is actually to hand is someone who decided to stop asking and is
willing to be named for having decided.

Adopting this makes stipulation respectable rather than embarrassing.
A design that treats "we assumed it" as a deficiency to be eliminated
spends its life eliminating the only thing that ever terminates.
