# Cheaper to Use Than to Ignore

The ledger competes with keeping no ledger, not with a better notation.
It is adopted only where recording a claim, referring to one, and
governing the set each cost less than the confusion they prevent — and
the comparison is made line by line, in the middle of doing something
else, by someone who did not set out to keep a ledger today.

This is the goal the economies serve: free entry with warrant charged at
transitions (cheap-entry-expensive-promotion.md), governance in one line
over labels (operations-fit-in-a-breath.md), no glyph a chat client
mangles (survives-dumb-media-and-tools.md).

It also settles feature arguments. Any proposal must answer who pays for
it on every line, not merely who benefits on the lines that use it.
