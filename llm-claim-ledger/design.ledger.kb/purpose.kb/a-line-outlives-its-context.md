# A Line Outlives Its Context

What a ledger is *for* is being read later, by someone who was not
there — most often an agent with no memory of the conversation that
produced the line. The ledger is worth more the less it must look
elsewhere.

This makes under-statement the characteristic failure, not verbosity. A
line needing an external lookup before it can be acted on has not
finished being written, and it fails silently: it reads fine to its
author, who still remembers, and is worthless to everyone after.

The goal is why claim text carries its own justification rather than
pointing at where the justification was said. Its operational form is
`../../SKILL.kb/write-for-a-fresh-reader.md`.
