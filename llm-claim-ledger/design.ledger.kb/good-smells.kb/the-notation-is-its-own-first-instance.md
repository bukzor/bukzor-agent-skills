# The Notation Is Its Own First Instance

This skill's commitments are recorded as claims, in this notation, with
labels and standing — one file each, here in `design.ledger.kb/`, sorted
into theories the way the notation says to sort them. That is not
decoration; it is the cheapest test available. A notation that cannot
carry its own design will not carry the user's, and the failure shows up
during authoring rather than after adoption.

Two things follow. Where the self-application lacks a feature the design
commits to, one of the two is wrong — a design claim nobody could be
bothered to apply is a claim to re-examine, not an aspiration. And a
term appearing at two fidelities — a chat line and a file, a label and a
filename, a sigil and a frontmatter field — is aligned, not colliding;
divergence between them is the smell, and renaming to dodge the overlap
hides exactly the correspondence worth checking.
