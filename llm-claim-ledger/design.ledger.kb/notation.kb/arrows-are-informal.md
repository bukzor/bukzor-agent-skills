# Arrows Are Informal

`<-` records what the writer had in view while committing to a claim.
It is not an entailment relation, nothing checks it, and it is not going
to become checkable later — the informality is permanent, not a stage.

The alternative was considered and declined. Checkable arrows require
committing to an inference relation, which requires every claim to be
stated in a language that relation is defined over. That cost lands
entirely on entry, the one place the design refuses to tax
(../good-smells.kb/cheap-entry-expensive-promotion.md), and it lands
hardest on exactly the half-formed claims most worth recording.

The arrows still earn their glyph by displaying premise standing where
weight is placed on it
(../good-smells.kb/standing-visible-at-point-of-use.md), and by giving
retraction a path to follow.
