# A Warrant Is a Discharged Stipulation

Warrant and stipulation are one mechanism at two stages -- a warrant
is a discharged stipulation (stipulation-is-a-legal-stop.md). That is
why `!` and `+` withdraw the same way, running the same propagation,
and why a discharged claim needs no signature at all: the check stands
where the judge stood (the-sigil-signs-the-judge.md).

Certified by review 089 in `prototype.personal-reasoning-management`,
the mechanized rung, whose `WARRANT` is this same commitment; the
certification transports because the content is shared. An earlier
version drew the opposite moral -- one sigil (`!`) covering both the
fiat-warranted and the checked -- which the-sigil-signs-the-judge.md
supersedes.
