# A Warrant Is a Discharged Stipulation

Warrant and stipulation are one mechanism at two stages — a warrant is
a discharged stipulation (stipulation-is-a-legal-stop.md). That is why
a single sigil (`!`) covers the fiat-warranted and the checked, and why
withdrawing either runs the same propagation.
