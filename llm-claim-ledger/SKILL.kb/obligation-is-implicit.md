# Obligation Is Implicit

Open claims need no debt declaration: each is obligated exactly to the
extent conclusions rest on it, and the warrant-mix at point of use
(`ZZ <- XY?`) shows where that is.

Name routes at discharge or flush; never demand them at entry. Asking
"how will you prove this?" of a claim as it is written is the tax that
stops claims being written.

At discharge or flush, fold the surviving opens into the fewest
questions that would close them -- opens cluster, and one answer often
closes several.
