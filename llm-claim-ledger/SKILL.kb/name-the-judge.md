# Name the Judge

Record every judgment with the judge that made it — `-- authority: user`,
`-- certified(pytest 8.2: 041 passed)`, `+` for one you made yourself.
An unattributed judgment is a hidden oracle, and hidden oracles are the
failure this notation exists to prevent.

The judge's name carries its version wherever the verdict could turn on
it: `certified(CHECK)` promises a *re-runnable* check, and a check whose
identity is "the linter" is not re-runnable.

Never accept your own output. `claim accept:` is the operator's call —
mark your own judgments `+` and let them stand until the user rules
(`stipulate-what-you-notice.md`).
