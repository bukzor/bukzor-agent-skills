# Importing Claims

The mirror of `flush.md`: claims arriving from outside the chat -- a
summary, a task file, another agent's report, your own prior session.

A conclusion that arrives without its argument enters as the
importer's judgment, not as fact: sign it `+` (or `?` if you would not
stand behind it) and note the absent argument -- `DTX+ <- (upstream
analysis, not restated)`. From there the normal paths apply: restate
the argument and it earns bare, the user accepts it and it carries
`!`, or neither and whatever rests on it stays priced.

What this prevents is laundering: a claim that was `?` upstream
arriving bare downstream because the transport stripped its standing.
