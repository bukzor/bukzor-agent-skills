# Retraction Propagates

On retracting `AB`, revisit every claim with `AB` as premise: re-derive
it, retract it in turn, or restate it without the lost support. Tell the
user what changed. Never silently keep a conclusion whose support
vanished.

Fiat warrant and check warrant propagate alike — withdraw the
stipulation or invalidate the check, and the same sweep applies
downstream.
