# The Recall Product Formula

The opening skeptical claim: sharding lowers recall because the
probability a member fires becomes the product of the gate firing and
the member firing. Retracted in debate -- it assumes per-trigger recall
is constant across contexts, which is exactly what a just-in-time
listing would change. The residue after both this and its opposite fell
is the single noticing premise, priced per occasion.
