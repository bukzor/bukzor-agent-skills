# A Begin Mid-Flow Is Missed

A begin that arrives while the agent is mid-flow -- responding to a
correction, editing -- is missed regardless of layout; one that arrives
during deliberate planning is caught. Same filename, same listing
distance, opposite outcomes on one trigger: 12/15 in a deliberating
state, 2/65 in a responding one.

Confounded, and known to be: the 63 misses were not split between
"listing present, not noticed" and "listing absent after compaction";
the sibling's follow-up test does that. Neither the listing's distance
nor its length is implicated either way. Generalization from one
trigger to activity occasions is the inference here, hence `agent`.

Stale when: an audit of a set's occasion shows misses correlating with
listing distance rather than with mode.
