# No New Gates, Ever

A draft rule: a set may be headed only by an existing top-level
`before/` trigger whose body grows one line. Rejected on the owner's
two objections -- it anchors on incumbency, which carries no warrant,
and it prohibits its own instances. What it was tracking survives as
the gate rule's second condition: the set's occasion must be noticed at
least as reliably as its members'.
