# A Mechanical Puller Discharges Noticing for Free

A mechanically pulled set never depends on the agent noticing; a
judgment-pulled set spends that premise. This is the whole content of
the early instinct "shard only along mechanical gates", and why it was
too strong: judgment pullers are admissible wherever the noticing
premise is cheap, which is what the gate rule prices.
