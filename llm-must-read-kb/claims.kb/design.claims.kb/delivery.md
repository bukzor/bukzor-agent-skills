# Delivery -- how a listing reaches an agent

Coins: the **listing command** is the one command that installs a set
at any depth, printing sub-sets as stubs; the **recognition line** is
the single sentence of host-level vocabulary that tells an agent what a
stub is and what to do when its occasion begins.

What this theory holds: the cost bound that makes stubs safe to show,
the command, where the recognition line lives, what wording does to a
directive's chance of being taken, and what compaction does to an
installed set.
