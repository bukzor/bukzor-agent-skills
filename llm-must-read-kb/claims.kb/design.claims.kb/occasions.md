# Occasions -- the preorder a bank is indexed by

Coins: an **occasion** is a predicate on moments; it **holds** at the
moments where it is true and **begins** at the first such moment; X
**entails** Y when Y holds at every moment X does; **top** holds at
every moment; the **meet** of two occasions is their conjunction.

The user's own definition in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` -- "an occasion is a
condition that may hold at any given moment" -- is the one stipulated
here, unchanged.
