# A Set Begins Before Its Members Fire

For every trigger T in the set at X, X begins no later than T fires. A
`before/` member fires at the moment preceding its action; deciding on
that action is deciding on X (entailment holds at the level of intent
as well as of moments), so X has already begun. `after/` and `when/`
members fire while or after their occasion holds, hence after X began.
