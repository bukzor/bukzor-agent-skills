# design.claims.kb/ -- must-read.kb's design commitments

## What belongs

A claim about how a trigger bank is shaped, what its paths mean, when
its directives install and fire, who installs them, or which occasions
earn a set. One claim per file, `kebab-case.md`, named in prose;
`label:` + `standing:` required; `why:` where a real warrant exists.

Where a new file goes: the outermost theory that coins every word its
text needs. A dissolved or retracted question is filed beside the claim
that dissolved it, with `verdict:`, not in a theory of its own.

## What does NOT belong

- The settled pattern, stated for a consumer -> `../../SKILL.md`
- A commitment binding llm-kb's base contract -> llm-kb's own ledger
- Condition vocabulary and mechanical delivery -> `Skill(llm-triggers)`'s
  `design.kb/`, which absorbs this ledger at the fold
- A task with an owner -> `../../.claude/todo.md`

## Standing, in this ledger

`open` is a question nobody has ruled. `agent` is the agent's call with
the veto window open -- edit the file to veto. `user` is the owner's,
sensatim in a `> [!@bukzor]` callout. `bare` needs no judge: it follows
from its `why:` or its `verify:` says how to check.
