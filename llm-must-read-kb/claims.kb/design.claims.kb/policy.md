# Policy -- which occasions earn a set, and where things live

Coins: an occasion **earns** a set when heading one is expected to
lower total listing cost without raising misses.

Everything above this theory is law or observation; everything here is
a judgment call the owner has made or may veto -- the gate rule, the
threshold, where a set's own point trigger sits, and where this ledger
lives.
