# Firing -- what a bank's paths mean

Coins: a **trigger** is a file under a point juncture, read as
(juncture, occasion, body); it **fires** at the moment its juncture
relates to its occasion -- `before` the moment preceding the action
that makes the occasion hold, `after` the moment following its end,
`when` any moment it holds; a trigger is **installed** at t when its
listing line is in context at t; to **install** a set is to list it.

The laws: a member entails its set; a set is installed when its
occasion begins; a trigger cannot gate the knowledge that fires it.
The theorem: every firing trigger is installed, resting on one open
premise -- that the agent notices the occasion begin.
