# A Set Earns Its Place on Cost and Precision

A set earns its place on a shorter top listing, clustering across
junctures, and one precision fix -- a turn-one sweep that `cat`s the
bank cannot reach a set's members -- and not on recall, which the
audits show is untouched by layout. Threshold: the occasion holds in a
minority of sessions and heads at least three triggers. An occasion
near top (`git`, `using-claude-code-tool`) stays namespace nesting,
because the pull would be paid every session for no saving; one or two
files stay flat.

Stale when: pull cost changes, the bank doubles, or an audit shows a
recall effect from layout.
