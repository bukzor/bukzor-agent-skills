# An Occasion May Head a Set When

An occasion X may head a set when, for every member:

1. **Entailment** -- the member's occasion entails X.
2. **Recall dominance** -- X's begin is noticed at least as reliably as
   the member's occasion. Satisfied by occasions that are broad and
   concrete -- an action, a tool, a cwd -- and violated by moods
   ("working on agent tooling").
3. **Timing** -- X begins no later than the earliest member fires.

(1) and (3) are laws in `../firing.kb/`; (2) is the noticing premise,
priced. This is the condition under which "a smaller list of
higher-level triggers has better recall" is true rather than hoped.
