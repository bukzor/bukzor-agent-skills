# fixpoint.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A theorem of order theory the tower leans on, stated so a downstream
claim can cite it by label. Standing is `bare`: these are checkable
facts, and a claim here that needs a judge is in the wrong theory.

## What does NOT belong here

Anything mentioning stores, claims, evidence, or files -- those words
live downstream. This theory must read correctly to a mathematician
who has never seen the ecosystem.
