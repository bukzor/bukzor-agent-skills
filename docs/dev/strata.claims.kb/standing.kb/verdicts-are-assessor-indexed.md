# Verdicts Are Assessor-Indexed

There is no assessor-free verdict. Every judgment of a claim -- a
party's endorsement, a checker's certificate, a ruling -- is issued
by someone or something, and holds relative to its issuer; "science
never proves" is this law's temporal face, since no issuer is final.
Parties and checkers are one sort: an assessor. Two consequences
follow structurally: verification's top splits into one point per
assessor (there is nothing above them all), and any store of
verdicts is a map indexed by who issued them.
