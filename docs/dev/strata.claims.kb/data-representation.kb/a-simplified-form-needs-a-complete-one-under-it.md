# A Simplified Form Needs a Complete One Under It

Simplified views and forms are fine and often good -- but only where
a complete representation underlies them. Sugar is licensed by the
full form it abbreviates, not by being convenient: without one, the
abbreviation *is* the carrier, and every structure it cannot express
is a structure the system cannot hold. ROUNDTRIP prices the loss a
poor target announces; this prices the loss no one can announce
because nothing richer was ever written down.

The ledger's own instance: `standing`'s four-point enum is sugar for
the assessor-keyed verdict map STANCE requires, and is legitimate in
exactly the degree that the map exists to desugar into.
