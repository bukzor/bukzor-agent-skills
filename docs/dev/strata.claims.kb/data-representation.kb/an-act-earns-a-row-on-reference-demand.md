# An Act Earns a Row on Reference-Demand

Acts are denoted from the first signed judgment -- an issuerless
status is a hidden oracle, not a simpler rung -- but an act rides
as fields on the claim it judges until something must point at it.
Then, and only then, it materializes as its own bare row,
content-addressed by what it already says: assessor, target,
occasion.

- The demand triggers are reference-shaped: contravention at a
  distance, a disputed certificate, a second assessor of the same
  sort on one claim.
- Editing a field in place is contravention with the address
  implicit -- the field is the address, the history is the record --
  so the common case costs no extra writing.
- The reified case costs one row exactly where a reference needs
  it, which is where the information is load-bearing.

SUGAR licenses the field form in exactly the degree the row form
exists to desugar into; this claim names when the desugaring is
obliged to happen.
