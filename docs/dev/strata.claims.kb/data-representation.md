persistence formats. This theory owes every structure a
representation in every live target and prices what each target
cannot do. It, not any lower theory, is where `file`, `directory`,
`frontmatter`, and `wire` are words -- the v1 skills are
filesystem-target instances (`fleet.kb/`), and a lower claim needing
these words is filed here or reworded.
