# Laws Outlive Their Motivations

State each law in the weakest vocabulary that supports it; proper
nouns, instances, deployments, and ambitions are quarantined in a
throwaway theory downstream, warranted upward by citation. The law
then survives its motivations: the named systems change or die, the
plans grow or shrink, and nothing upstream moves. This is
confinement applied to motive -- a theory admits no vocabulary its
laws do not need, and what a theory never heard of cannot defeat it.
