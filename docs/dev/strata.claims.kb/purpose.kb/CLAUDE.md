# purpose.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

An operating-regime requirement: a property of the corpus's scale,
economics, or representational duty that laws elsewhere must
survive. These are the user's stipulations -- standing `user` is the
natural resting state.

## What does NOT belong here

Proper nouns, deployments, or ambitions -- instances of the regime
are quarantined downstream (`../fleet.kb/`, or the devlog), per
`../genre.kb/laws-outlive-their-motivations.md`. If a requirement
needs a named system to state, it is not regime; it is fleet.
