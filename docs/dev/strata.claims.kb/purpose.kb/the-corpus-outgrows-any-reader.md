# The Corpus Outgrows Any Reader

The corpus grows without bound -- more claims, more contributors,
more years -- and no reader, human or machine, holds all of it. No
law may therefore require per-change work proportional to corpus
size: the unit of review stays bounded while the corpus does not. A
law that scales with the corpus is not a law but a deadline.
