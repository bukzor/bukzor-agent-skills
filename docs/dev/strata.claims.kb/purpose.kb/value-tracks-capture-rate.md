# Value Tracks Capture Rate

The corpus is worth what it captures. Every gate at entry -- a
schema to satisfy, a formality to learn, a review to await -- is
paid in claims that were never written down, and the loss is silent:
nothing marks the absence. A corpus that loses most claims at the
door is a failed system regardless of the quality of what got in.
Entry cost stays near zero; quality is raised after capture, not
demanded before it.
