# Retraction Needs an Update Form

A log only appends, yet theories downstream lean on taking back --
removal, reversal, a thing once held and held no longer. Which
update form realizes taking back is unspecified, and the candidates
are different stores: an override that shadows the key, so the fold
forgets while the log remembers; a retraction update naming what it
voids, so voiding becomes content every reader must honor; editing
the word itself, which LOG forbids. Until one is filed, every
downstream appeal to removal names a law this stratum has not
supplied.
