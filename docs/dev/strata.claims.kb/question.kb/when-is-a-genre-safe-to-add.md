# When Is a Genre Safe to Add?

As wrestled (PRMS's CLAIMS_ONLY and GENERIC_BASE; `design-next.kb/`'s
core-and-classes rules): how can many genres share one core without
each new genre silently changing what old entries mean?

Settled up to one proof: a genre is safe iff its rules are monotone
(the ascent-only format, fixed once in the base) and confined
(conclusions only on its own entries) -- checkable syntactically,
before anything runs. Residue: OBLIGATION -- the universal theorem is
stated, instance-witnessed, and unproved.
