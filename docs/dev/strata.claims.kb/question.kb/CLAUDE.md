# question.kb -- maintenance guide

The historical theory: the core design problems the rest of the
ledger was bought to settle. Historically first -- everything
downstream is their decomposition; logically last -- stating what
decomposes a question takes the whole tower's vocabulary.
`../question.md` is the synthesis.

- `prior:` fleet
- `ontology:` question, posing, wrestling, settlement, dissolution,
  residue
- `defeated by:` the user disavowing a question, or a driving
  question the tower cannot decompose

## What belongs here

One claim per driving problem: the question as wrestled (proper
nouns welcome as past-tense provenance -- history, so `fleet`'s
defeater does not reach it), the well-posed form where re-posing was
the settlement, what decomposes it, what residue stays open. These
are claims about the user's own questions -- agent-signed
reconstructions, the natural place for a `!` ratification.

## What does NOT belong here

The answers themselves -- state them upstream and cite them. Design
problems still being wrestled -> `design-next.kb/` until they have a
decomposition to claim.
