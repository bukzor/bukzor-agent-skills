what became the top of the theory poset), the retention question in
the user's own words now that the theorem shapes are known, and
pre-registration -- the bets are what make 070's agreements evidence
instead of politeness. A miss looks like questions stated only in
their well-posed form, with the as-experienced half skipped.

Last, place your bets: which of your claims do you expect to survive comparison with the answer I'm holding, which do you expect it beats you on, and where do you expect it found something you couldn't have? On the record, before the reveal -- that's what makes the comparison mean anything.
