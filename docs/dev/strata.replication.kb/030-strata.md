# 030 -- Stratify

Send when the survivors all sit at one level of generality -- in the
original this was a correction after the fact; here it is the shape
of the analysis. A miss looks like claims about "the engine" with no
answer to which engine.

````
stratify: we have several layers of "engine" here; a claim true of the lowest, most-generic engine may miss richer structure in the higher ones -- and a claim about a higher one may be false of the base.

So build the levels explicitly: what makes a level a level, what relation orders them, and which of your surviving structures lives at which level -- laws re-checked against that level's own data. Genericity and structure trade against each other; locate every claim on that trade.

Where one level's structure is definable from the level below, say so -- that's load-bearing, not trivia.
````
