# Devlog: 2026-08-20 — The trespass docket falls from 253 to 9

## Focus

The corpus scan shipped with 253 word findings over 122 stipulations.
The user asked the only question that mattered:

> how many of those findings actually indicate a violation of the
> other theory's primacy or orthogonality? where the answer is "none",
> we should just remove the claim. where the answer is "some" we can
> consider making the claim more specific.

Every citation was read. Nine were violations. The other 244 fell in
four passes, each of which taught something different.

## The four passes

**253 → 145: a stipulation that fires on English.** The great majority
of findings were the ordinary word, not another theory using the
owner's sense: `standing` as "a standing invitation", `reach` as
"reach for", `force` as "forces the judgment", `contention` as "the
locus of contention", `tool` as "a chat client with no files and no
tools". Roughly forty entries came out of thirteen defining claims.

The structural fact underneath is worth keeping: **the words that
trespass are the ones too common to own, and the words that never
trespass are exactly the coinages.** `conservativity`,
`linearization`, `dissolution`, `idle import` never fired once. A
stipulation excludes every sibling from a word, so it is only worth
making for a word the theory coined.

**145 → 123: a word the whole ledger says.** In
`llm-claims/design.claims.kb/`, `notation` owned `label`, `sigil`,
`premise`, `warrant`, `arrow`, `judge`, `check`, `standing`, `status`,
`stipulation`, `theory`, `prior` — and every sibling is *about* the
notation, so every sibling said them. The stipulation was not wrong,
it was filed one level too low. Those words, plus `ledger` and
/deformalize's `glossary`, moved up to their ledgers' defining claims,
where the ontology is ambient. `notation` keeps the vocabulary of the
decisions themselves: node type, veto, signature, scan, staleness
condition.

**123 → 19: DIRECT_ONLY was costing 104 findings.** The word sort's
`importers` truncated reach at one hop, while the label sort of the
same law resolved through the chain. OWNERSHIP is stale when a shipped
scan disagrees with these laws, and one law answering the same
question two ways for its two sorts is exactly that. Settled by the
record rather than by fiat, and settled transitive: a taker has to be
able to read what it rests on, and a prior wrote its claims in its own
priors' words, so a one-hop reach leaves a taker unable to read its
own premises. `DIRECT_ONLY` became `IMPORT_CHAIN`.

The open question had been measured before and looked cheap — of 92
label citations, none resolved through the chain alone. That probe
measured the wrong sort. The word sort had 104 riding on it.

**19 → 9: the six imports the prose was already using.** `fleet` is
the proper-noun theory that places named systems in every stratum, so
it reads `data-structures` as it reads the tower; `question` indexes
what every stratum was bought to settle; `record` calls a
hand-restated migration a cache; `standing` cites grade escalation as
what found its gap; `data-representation` names the seam it sits
above; `principles` argues from the hedge channel and from what routes
a reader.

## The law the directions teach

The finding's position in the import poset picks its repair, and the
three cases are exhaustive:

- **Above** the owner — the site reaches it through a chain. Licensed;
  there was never a finding.
- **Across** — neither imports the other. Add the import. One line in
  `why:`, no prose touched, and the arrow was true already.
- **Below** — the owner already imports the site. The import repair is
  not cumbersome here, it is *unavailable*: the licensing arrow would
  close a cycle. This is the only shape a violation can take, and it
  is the shape ENGINE's staleness condition names.

Promotion is the wrong instrument for the third case, and the contrast
with the second pass is the whole point. In `llm-claims/design` the
words really were the ledger's, so making them ambient stated a truth.
In `strata` the ledger's staleness condition *is* "a lower engine
needing a higher vocabulary" — making the word ambient does not repair
the reach, it makes it unmeasurable. It buys the scan's silence by
deleting the property the scan exists to measure.

## What is left

Nine findings, all upward reach, awaiting the user's ruling. Four want
a reword, two a `uniquify`, one moves a word down to `fixpoint` where
its term of art lives, one moves a claim up into `tower.kb/`, and one
— `sort`, which `standing` uses in genre's many-sorted sense from
below genre — is a real question about where `genre` sits.
