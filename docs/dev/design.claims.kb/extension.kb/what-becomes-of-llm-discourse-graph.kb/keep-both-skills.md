# Keep Both Skills

Leave `llm-discourse-graph` as a peer format and let the two coexist.
Rejected on parsimony: two skills over the same ground are a
maintenance, understandability, and usability nightmare.

Appeal: zero migration, and the two vocabularies do serve different
habits of work. Cost: the reason for two skills was a truth-apt vs
held-desired split that dissolved once standing signed the judge, so
what remains is duplication -- and the graph's stored likelihoods
stay unlawful for as long as the skill stays live.
