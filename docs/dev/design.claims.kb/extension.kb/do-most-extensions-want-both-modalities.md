# Do Most Extensions Want Both Modalities?

BASIS is ruled for the discourse-graph successor. The hunch behind
it -- that most extensions would do the same, taking both modalities
rather than binding themselves to one -- is explicitly unwarranted:
"I have no basis for this hunch" (2026-08-16).

Cheapest discharge is a second instance. Until one exists, BASIS
stands as a ruling about one extension, not a rule about extensions.
