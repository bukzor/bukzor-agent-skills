# What Need Does a Stored Likelihood Serve?

**Assertion and hedging want separate channels.** An agent that has
to carry its uncertainty in the prose hedges the prose; give it
somewhere else to put the uncertainty and the sentence can be written
flat.

> [!@bukzor]
>
> Thinking back on why i added/requested this, I think it's because I got so tired of LLM being so un-assertive.
> If we give them room to assert then separately hedge, the writing doesn't need hedging.

That is the need. Whether `likelihood: 0.7` is a good answer to it is
two further questions, in
`what-need-does-a-stored-likelihood-serve.kb/`. Neither blocks
anything: the field is carried over unchanged until they close --
"We just keep the field until it's closed."
