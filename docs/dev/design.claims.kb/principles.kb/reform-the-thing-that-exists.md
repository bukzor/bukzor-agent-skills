# Reform the Thing That Exists

Improve by reforming what stands, not by multiplying artifacts:
build one skill where two would overlap, reform in place rather
than replace, and make any proposed second thing earn its keep
against the merged one, not against nothing. The hard edge of this
preference is its own rule:
`whoever-meets-the-old-form-is-routed.md`.
