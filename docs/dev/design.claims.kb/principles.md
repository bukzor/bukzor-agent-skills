# principles -- the fleet's constitution

The few timeless design rules the fleet's decisions derive from, one
imperative per file, each graded `force:`: `must` filters the design
space -- violating work is non-conforming; `should` sorts it --
deviation takes recorded justification. A decision a principle
motivates needs no record of its own; a recurring decision no
principle motivates is a missing principle.

Extracted from the 2026-08-16/17 batch by an alignment census
(`/align`); the census itself lives in git history. Each principle's
`why:` arrows point at the decisions that embody it -- evidence of
extraction, not entailment -- and its standing is the ruling it has
actually received: `agent` invites veto as ever.
