# tower.kb -- maintenance guide

The capstone: what the strata form when you look at them together.

- `prior:` genre, protocol
- `ontology:` stratum, tower, seam, stratification, self-application
- `defeated by:` a lower engine needing a higher vocabulary -- one
  genuine upward reach and the stratification claim is dead

## What belongs here

A commitment about the tower as a whole: its shape, its
self-similarity, what remains unproved about it.

## What does NOT belong here

Claims internal to one stratum (file them there), and proper nouns
(-> `../fleet.kb/`).
