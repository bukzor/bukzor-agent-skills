# Confinement Is the Syntactic Half

Confinement -- a claim may use only the vocabulary its theory admits,
own plus priors' -- is what gives every claim a home signature, making
the space of claims functorial in vocabulary: restrict the vocabulary
and you know exactly which claims survive. Without it, "which theory
does this claim live in" has no answer and nothing downstream that
quantifies over a theory's claims is well-defined.
