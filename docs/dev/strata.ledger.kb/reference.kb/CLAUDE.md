# reference.kb -- maintenance guide

The graph stratum: entries citing entries, and everything order
theory gives you over that.

- `prior:` record, fixpoint
- `ontology:` edge, quiver, path, reachability, preorder, cone,
  up-set, integrity, weight, semiring, provenance, support, taint
- `defeated by:` references a machine cannot enumerate -- links living
  only in prose defeat every computation claimed here

## What belongs here

A commitment about the derived graph structure of an instance:
integrity, reachability, cones, weighted generalizations.

## What does NOT belong here

What an edge *means* epistemically (warrant, defeat) -- that is
`../standing.kb/` and beyond. This theory treats edges as bare
structure.
