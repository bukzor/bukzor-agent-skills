# history.kb -- maintenance guide

The substrate stratum: what a store is before any content is
interpreted.

- `prior:` -- none
- `ontology:` update, history, word, transaction, branch, store,
  state, key, payload, override, fold, query
- `defeated by:` a substrate that forgets its past -- mutation in
  place with no recoverable history

## What belongs here

A commitment about histories, states, and the relation between them
that holds for any store regardless of what the entries mean.

## What does NOT belong here

Anything about what entries contain (schemas -> `../record.kb/`),
what they cite (-> `../reference.kb/`), or values derived from state
(-> `../view.kb/`).
