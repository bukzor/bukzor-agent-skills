# The Floor Is the Semantics

An entry means what its informal statement says to a reader with no
tooling at all -- on every consumer, always. That floor reading is
not a fallback; it is the semantics. A binding -- a matcher, a test,
a proof -- raises the enforcement grade of that same meaning and
never reinterprets it, so an unbound entry is not broken but at base
strength, and partial formalization is the designed steady state.
Entry stays at prose cost.

The prose is authoritative, not infallible: a binding that disagrees
with its prose impeaches it, and the correction is recorded as a
prose amendment -- never as the binding silently meaning something
else.
