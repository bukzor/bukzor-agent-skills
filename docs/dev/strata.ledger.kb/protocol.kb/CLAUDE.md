# protocol.kb -- maintenance guide

The enforcement stratum: rules that fire on situations, and who makes
them fire.

- `prior:` history, view
- `ontology:` trigger, condition, directive, bank, monitor, guard,
  product, agent, enforcement, grade, runtime, interpretation, adapter
- `defeated by:` a runtime that enforces every rule natively -- banks
  dissolve into the kernel and this stratum becomes its
  configuration format

## What belongs here

A commitment about trigger banks as formal objects: their semantics,
their interpretations, the grades of enforcement.

## What does NOT belong here

The content of any particular rule, or which strata the situations
mention -- a condition may read any state; this theory only says what
a bank *is*.
