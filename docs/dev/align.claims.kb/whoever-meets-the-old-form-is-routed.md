# Whoever Meets the Old Form Is Routed

Any change must leave a live route for the reader who meets the old
form cold: whatever they need next -- guide, successor, trigger --
must be reachable from the thing they were already going to load,
which is why names and locations survive a reform. A plan that
removes the routing artifact strands that reader and is
non-conforming however large its subtraction -- this is the filter
that rejected deleting `llm-discourse-graph` outright.
