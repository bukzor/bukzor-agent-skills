# A Written Projection Is Cache or Testimony

A projection written down has two lawful lives, chosen at write
time. As a cache it stays owned by its view: stamped with what it
derives from, re-derivable at will, refreshed or honestly stale --
the triangle's regime. As testimony it joins the log: attributed and
dated, never refreshed, its worth being what was derived *then* -- a
record, which later change cannot rot, only supersede. The unmarked
middle is the unlawful case: a document read as current and
maintained as neither, refreshed by hand sometimes and stamped
never, is a standing invitation to read stale as fresh.

The ledger's own instances: `question.md`'s table declares itself
cache by carrying its view function; run records and devlogs are
testimony, which is why they keep superseded vocabulary unedited.
