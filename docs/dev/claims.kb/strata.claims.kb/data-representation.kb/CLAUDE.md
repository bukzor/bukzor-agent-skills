# data-representation.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment about how a structure lands in a target: the target
roster and its prices, coverage, faithfulness of a transport, what a
target's collation or enumerability does to the laws above.

## What does NOT belong here

Which structures exist (-> `../data-structures.kb/`), laws over the
structures themselves (-> the central theories), correspondence with
named systems (-> `../fleet.kb/`).
