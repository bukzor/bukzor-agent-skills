# A child agent's carriers are its own bootstrap plus the brief

A child agent's carriers are its own bootstrap plus the brief. Nothing in
the parent's context propagates, so a directive the parent holds is absent in
the child unless the brief restates it. The three extraction subagents of
2026-09-01 received CLAUDE.md as a source to quote and returned lists; none was
positioned to derive.
