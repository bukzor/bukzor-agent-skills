# Which enforcement grades a juncture admits is decided by its kind

A hook is the tooling grade of enforcement and reaches only observable
junctures, where it needs no recognition and costs no tokens. Prose in context
is the attention grade and reaches semantic junctures too, but only through
recognition. The grade is the protocol theory's word for which machine computes
the monitor's product; what this claim adds is that the juncture's kind bounds
which grades are available at all. Forcing a semantic juncture onto an
observable proxy, such as any write into a `.kb` directory, raises the grade at
the price of false fires, and that price is measurable.
