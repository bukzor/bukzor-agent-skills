# carriage.kb -- maintenance guide

`../carriage.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about how a directive reaches an agent's context at the moment it governs, and what that costs, confined to the words `../carriage.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim about what a rule says, or about the owner: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
