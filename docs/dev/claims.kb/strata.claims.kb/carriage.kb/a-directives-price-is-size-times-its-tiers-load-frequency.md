# A directive's price is its size times its carrier's load frequency

A directive's price is its size times the load frequency of its carrier's
tier, plus the price of every body it delegates to. A hook prices zero tokens
and nonzero latency. The tiers and the delegation charge are the authorship
ledger's ruling of 2026-08-10, imported here rather than restated.
