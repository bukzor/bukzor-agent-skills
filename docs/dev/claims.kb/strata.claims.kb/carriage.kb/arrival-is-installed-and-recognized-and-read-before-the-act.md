# A directive arrives when installed, recognized, and read before the act

A directive arrives when it is installed, its juncture is recognized, and
its body is read before the act. Each conjunct fails separately: an absent
carrier, an unrecognized juncture, a body read after the act. The four or five
law-class violations after the acts-not-laws line entered CLAUDE.md were all
recognition failures at semantic junctures.
