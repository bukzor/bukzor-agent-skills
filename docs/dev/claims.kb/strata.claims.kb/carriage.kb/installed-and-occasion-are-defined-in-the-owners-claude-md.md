# Installed and occasion are defined in the owner's CLAUDE.md

A trigger is installed once its listing is in context; an occasion is a
condition that may hold at any moment; `before` marks a dependency. These are
the owner's definitions in the Required Reading stanza of `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`
and this theory reuses them rather than coining rivals.
