# The Courts Are the Sigils

`review-open-questions` and `llm-claims` present one verdict
taxonomy at two enforcement grades. The skill's courts sort an open
to the cheapest competent judge; the ledger's sigils record which
judge settled. Settled by the record is a citation of a standing
`!`; by check is bare (`certified`); by witness is `+` resting under
standing veto; the owner's court issues `!`; an open question is
`?`. The species rule -- an open is a veto or a question by what
silence does -- is the `+`/`?` distinction, and the skill's "a
signature adds nothing a witness didn't" is the notation's
residual-choice rule restated. One is a procedure held by attention,
the other a notation held by grep: the assessor law and the status
order, worn twice.

Neither skill may cite the other (the fleet's authorship rules), so
this claim is where the alignment is kept: a change to either
taxonomy gets checked against it here.
