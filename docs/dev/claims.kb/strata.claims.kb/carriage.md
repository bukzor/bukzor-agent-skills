# carriage -- how a rule reaches an agent at the moment it governs

A rule governs only where its directive is in the agent's context, its
juncture is recognized, and its body was read before the act. Carriers differ
in what they can reach and what they cost, and the assignment of rules to
carriers is the whole of an instruction-layer rewrite.
