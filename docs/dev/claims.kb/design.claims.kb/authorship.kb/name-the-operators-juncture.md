# Name the Operator's Juncture

Where a skill defers to law the operator keeps in their own bank, it
names the occasion at which that law fires -- "composing this turn is
asking the user" -- and stops there. The operator's trigger system is
the wiring; naming the occasion is the whole reference.

- The law stays where the operator can rewrite it without a fleet
  release; the skill carries the stance that such law exists and
  where in the turn it binds.
- A path into one operator's home would break the skill for every
  other operator, and a fleet skill has no home to point into.
- An enumeration of what the entry contains would restate it: the
  list goes stale the moment the entry changes, and re-imports the
  duplication that keeping the law in one place removed.

This is BINDINGS at the fleet's edge: the situational half is named
under one heading, and here the heading is the occasion. Worked
instance: `review-open-questions`, rebuilt 2026-08-29
(`../../../devlog/2026-08-29-000-The-sweep-sheds-the-asking-law.md`),
which names the juncture and enumerates nothing. Declined: a
fleet-carried copy of the operator's asking law, deferred until an
external consumer or a second fleet-wide entry needs it.
