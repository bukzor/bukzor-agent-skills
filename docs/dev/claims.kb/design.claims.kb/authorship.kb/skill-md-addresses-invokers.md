# SKILL.md addresses invokers only

The body of a skill speaks to the agent that just loaded it -- the
one about to do the task. Rules for the skill's *authors* (citation
discipline, naming grammar, style) are noise there: the invoker pays
their tokens on every load and can act on none of them, while authors
rarely reread the body they are editing. Author-facing rules live on
the author's side of the fence -- this ledger, `../../adr/` -- where
invokers never pay for them.
