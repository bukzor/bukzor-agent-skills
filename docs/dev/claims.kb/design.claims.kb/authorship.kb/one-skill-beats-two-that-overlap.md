# One skill beats two that overlap

Two skills covering the same ground cost three times over, not twice:
maintenance for the author, and both understandability and usability
for the invoker, who has to decide which one this situation is before
reading either.

> [!@bukzor]
>
> this would be a maintenance, understandability, usability nightmare

So a proposed second skill earns its keep against the merged one, not
against nothing. "They serve different habits of work" does not
clear that bar -- differing habits are what an extension's added
ontology is for.
