# Price Text by Load Frequency

Skill text sorts into tiers by how often it is loaded, and each tier
has its own objective; optimizing by another tier's objective wastes
the work or charges the fleet.

- **Loaded every session** -- names, descriptions, any block mirrored
  into always-on preferences. Every token recurs forever
  (the-invoker-pays-for-every-token.md); the objective is the fewest
  tokens that still trigger and still operate.
- **Loaded per invocation** -- the body, and the entries it points
  to, read by an invoker mid-task. The objective is the invoker's
  next action: exemplars over policy, triggers over topics.
- **Loaded to be argued with** -- design ledgers, ADRs, devlogs: the
  author's side of the fence (skill-md-addresses-invokers.md). The
  cheapest tokens in the fleet, so brevity is the wrong objective;
  optimize for extraction and address -- the commitment pullable
  without error, a sentence for every contest to point at.

The tell of a misfiled optimization: compressing cold text saves
tokens nobody was paying, while elaborating hot text charges every
invoker forever.
