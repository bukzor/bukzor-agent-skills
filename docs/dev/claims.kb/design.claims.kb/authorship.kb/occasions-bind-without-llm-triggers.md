# Occasions Bind Through Must-Read Entries and `triggers:`

An occasion binds to its payload through a `must-read.kb/` entry named for it,
and through `triggers:` frontmatter where must-read does not reach. Plans assume
`llm-triggers` arrives at no foreseeable date, and perhaps never.
