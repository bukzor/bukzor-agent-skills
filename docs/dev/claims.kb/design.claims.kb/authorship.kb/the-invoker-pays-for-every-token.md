# The invoker pays for every token

A skill's text is read at two metered moments, both by agents who did
not write it: retrieval time, when every session scans names and
descriptions deciding what to load, and load time, when the body
lands in the invoker's context. The author pays once, at writing;
invokers pay on every invocation, forever. This asymmetry is the
economics the other authorship claims price against: anything in a
skill that its invoker cannot act on is a recurring charge with no
payer benefit.
