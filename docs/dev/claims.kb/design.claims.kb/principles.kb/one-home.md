# One Home per Conclusion and per Reasoning

Each conclusion, and the reasoning for it, has exactly one home, and everything
else links to it. A copy is a second home, free to drift; a cut that removes the
last statement of something is a loss, not a subtraction. A carrier that
delivers a rule at its occasion -- a `CLAUDE.md`, a trigger, a skill description
-- names the act and links to the home, and the home wins where they differ.

A conclusion and its reasoning may have different homes -- a rule in a policy
file, its argument in a design ledger -- each citing the other, neither
restating it.
