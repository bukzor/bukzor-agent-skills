# An Open Is Settled in the Cheapest Competent Court

Anything that can settle an open is a court, and the courts order by
cost: the record, a check, a witness, and dearest of all the
operator. Settle every open in the cheapest court competent to settle
it. A settlement from the wrong court is void in both directions: a
fact ratified by the operator records a false judge, and a
stipulation defaulted by an agent is a veto still waiting.

- The sigils are the courts' seal (SIGNATURE): bare where the record
  or a check settled it, `+` where a witness did, `!` where only the
  operator could, `?` where none has yet.
- The economics are LOAD_COST's: the operator's attention is the
  scarcest budget the fleet spends, and a question whose answer the
  record already holds spends it for nothing.
- WARRANT is the same law read from the claim's side: a claim is
  worth its evidence, so the court that supplied the evidence is the
  court that settled it.

Embodied in `review-open-questions`, whose sweep exhausts the cheap
courts before presenting a batch, and in `llm-claims-kb`'s rule that
a candidate claim is derived from the record before it is extracted
from an instance. Declined: the operator as the default court, every
open presented and every ruling by fiat -- respectful in form, and at
any scale a denial of service on the one court no agent can replace.
