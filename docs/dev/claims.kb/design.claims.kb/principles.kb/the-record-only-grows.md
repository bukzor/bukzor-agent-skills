# The Record Only Grows

Change the record by adding to it, never by erasing it. A negative
verdict is a new mark on the loser: a rejected proposal keeps its
body whole -- its worth is why it lost -- while a retracted claim
may be rewritten into its supersession notice, the git diff keeping
the strike. What is absent is accepted: growth of the
notation obliges no rewrite of existing claims, a reforming skill
obliges no rewrite of its instances, and old formats stay legal
indefinitely. Delete nothing, move nothing, rewrite nothing on a
schedule.
