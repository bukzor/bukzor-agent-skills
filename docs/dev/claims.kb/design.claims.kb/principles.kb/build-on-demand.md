# Build on Demand, Not on Speculation

Build structure when a real case demands it and not before. Where
escalation is foreseeable, plant a tripwire and act only when it
fires; until then hold the simple form, even when the complete form
is already named. A question that blocks nothing waits at leisure.
Each deferral is recorded beside the open question it holds back,
in that question's own ledger -- the `why:` arrows point at them.
