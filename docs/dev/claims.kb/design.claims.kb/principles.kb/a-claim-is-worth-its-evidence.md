# A Claim Is Worth Exactly Its Evidence

Cap every claim at its warrant, and show the warrant: `why:` arrows
to the priors that actually bear, `bare` standing where nothing
signs. An open question closes only by ruling, never by momentum --
and open may mean open forever.
