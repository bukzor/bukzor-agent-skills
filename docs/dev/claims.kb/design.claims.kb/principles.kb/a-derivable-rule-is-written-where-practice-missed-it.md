# A Derivable Rule Is Written Down Where Practice Missed It

Logic says what follows from the record; practice says what a reader actually
derives at the point of use. They diverge where the premises sit far from the
use, on separate branches, or where a miss is costly. Once practice shows a
consequence missed, it is written where it is used, as a corollary: it cites its
premises in `why:`, which stay the home of its reasoning (`ONE_HOME`), and it
names the miss that earned it, so a later pass can tell a corollary from a
restatement.
