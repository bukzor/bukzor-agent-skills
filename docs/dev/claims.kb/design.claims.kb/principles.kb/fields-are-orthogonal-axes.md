# Fields Are Orthogonal Axes

Each field carries one independent question, and growth follows the
axis: a new value of an existing axis is a new word in its field; a
new axis is a new field. Never split one axis across fields (a
boolean per phenomenon), and never smuggle a second axis into a
shared field (tuple values). Standard data engineering, affirmed
rather than invented. Pricing is the other half of the decision:
orthogonality says what shape growth takes; who-pays-on-every-line
says whether to grow at all.

The batch's own instances: `verdict:` entered lawfully as a new
field, because outcome is its own axis, and its values are words;
the `defeated:`/`dissolved:` booleans failed as one axis split
across two fields; the hedge channel is the same move at chat scale
-- assertion and uncertainty each on their own axis.
