# A ruling's record is the owner's words at their transcript line

A ruling's record is the owner's words with the transcript line they came
from. The same words without a line are a claim about the ruling and carry the
speaker's standing, not the owner's. The 2026-09-03 review attributed three
agent sentences to the owner this way, each caught only by reading the role off
the raw record.
