# commitment.kb -- maintenance guide

`../commitment.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about what the owner holds and how a ruling becomes a record, confined to the words `../commitment.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim about carriers, junctures, or asks: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
