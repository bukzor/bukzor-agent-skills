# A Paraphrase Keeps the Ruling's Scope

A claim may word a ruling in the agent's voice, never at a different scope:
every condition, qualifier, and alternative the owner attached survives into the
claim. A narrowed paraphrase is worse than a stated disagreement, since the
claim still cites the ruling, so the owner's authority covers words the owner
never said, and nothing on the page shows the change.

The receipt: in my-own-shadertoy (`github.com/bukzor-sandbox/my-own-shadertoy`), session 59b55f01, 2026-11-23..25, "as long as requirements clearly serve goals (or some
higher requirement, i suppose), that's fine" was filed as "checks a goal here".
The owner caught it.
