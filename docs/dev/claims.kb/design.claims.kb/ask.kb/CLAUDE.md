# ask.kb -- maintenance guide

`../ask.md` defines this theory and stipulates its words.

## What belongs here

One claim per file about the move that hands a decision to the owner: its shape, its cost to the reader, its priority, confined to the words `../ask.md` and
its containers admit.

## What does NOT belong here

A claim about carriers or about simulation machinery: that is another theory's, and a claim needing its words imports
it through `why:`.

## When

Add a file when a claim in this vocabulary is made, contested, or ruled.
