# adjudication -- how a ruling arrives, and how it becomes law

> [!DRAFT] superseded 2026-09-09 by `commitment.md`, which derives the same
> subject from the owner's rulings at their transcript addresses; the owner's
> 2026-09-02 finding was that this collection was written upward from
> anecdotes rather than downward from those rulings.

The owner's court, as law: what counts as a ruling, the forms one may
arrive in, and the one way an instance becomes standing law. A
**court** is anything that can settle an open -- the record, a check,
a witness, dearest of all the owner -- ordered by cost; **graduation**
is an instance ruling becoming law that binds the next case. Claims
here bind every surface and register in this repo that takes rulings:
the law between the skills, not any skill's procedure.

What this theory does not keep: the alignment of courts with the
notation's sigils is COURTS's charter; the record algebra under both
is standing's theory; how each surface composes its asking is that
surface's own prose, audited against these claims; and the operator's
personal asking law (the homedir bank) outranks this register and is
cited, never restated. On record a ruling has arrived as a pick, as a
universal applied back to its batch, as criteria to compute from, as
a rejection of the question itself, and as a counter-question
deferring the decision -- the members hold the forms that carry law
of their own.
