# A Claim Is Born in Its Durable Ledger

Extraction and review write each claim directly into the ledger whose
ontology admits it -- new claims at `standing: agent`, questions at
`open` -- and mint no review-shaped ledger for the occasion. The
notation already carries review state on every claim (`agent` invites
veto by definition), and git already owns staging: the diff is the
batch's scope, reverting it is wholesale rejection, merging it is
reconciliation complete, and the standing scan answers what awaits
ruling long after the diff is gone. A staged copy elsewhere adds no
capability and bills the fold -- every claim moved home after ruling.

The defeated incumbent: the 2026-08-16/17 alignment census
(`align.claims.kb/`, b7b89c0), which restaged claims beside their
homes. Its residue proved redundant with the durable ledgers and its
teardown cost a session (ae1dee3). APPEND_ONLY had already priced the
lesson: what is never staged never needs moving.
