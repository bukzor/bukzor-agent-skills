# Merit Outranks Incumbency

A conflict between contenders -- two names, two forms, incumbent text
against a newcomer -- settles by re-deriving from the governing law
at the consumer's position. Seniority, arrival order, and authorship
are not grounds; they are how a defect stays canonical.

- Re-derived, the senior side may simply lose: in the ruling above
  "every criterion inverts the answer" -- the elder claim had been
  misnamed "since it was minted, which the collision merely exposed",
  and the switching cost "was never load-bearing"
  (`../../../devlog/2026-08-31-000-The-rule-was-already-written--what-was-missing-was-its-arrival.md`).
- Effectiveness outranks incumbency: where one variant has proven to
  work and the broader incumbent has not, "the proven name becomes
  canonical" (ADR 2026-08-29-001).

The record's algebra already refuses seniority at its own level:
assessor identity carries no force (BLIND), and recency resolves
nothing (EXPLICIT). This claim carries the refusal from acts to
artifacts: incumbency is an address, not a warrant.

Declined: tiebreak by arrival order and re-signing cost -- the exact
recommendation the correction reversed.
