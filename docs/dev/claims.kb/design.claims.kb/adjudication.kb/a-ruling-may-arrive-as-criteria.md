# A Ruling May Arrive as Criteria

The owner may settle an open by naming the criteria that decide it
rather than the outcome; the agent computes what the criteria entail,
and the result stands as the owner's ruling. The ruling was the
choice of grounds -- the computation only discharges it.

The standing splits exactly as COURTS sorts it: the criteria carry
the owner's word, while the entailment is the agent's reading of
them, presented and resting under standing veto like any witness. A
wrong computation is a failed witness -- re-present it; the criteria
stand untouched.

Declined: bouncing the answer -- treating named criteria as a
non-answer and re-asking for a verdict. That bills the dearest court
twice for one open, and asks a judge to hand-compute what is now a
check's work; the entailment is worth the criteria plus the
computation, and needs no second fiat (WARRANT).

Worked instance: the authority ruling above -- three named criteria,
one computed entailment ("Homes, by ecosystem" over a second strategy
vocabulary), filed as ruled the same day.
