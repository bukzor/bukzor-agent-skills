# The Owner May Reject the Question Itself

A verdict may land on the question rather than within it: the owner's
court also judges whether an open is well-formed, and a malformed
question returns unruled -- the return is a ruling, not a refusal.
Two malformations are on record:

- **It asks a fact.** An answer entailed by premises already granted
  belongs to the record or a check (COURTS), and putting it to the
  owner records a false judge however graciously they answer.
- **It cannot be ruled alone.** A question answerable only after its
  siblings is not yet a question; present the cluster as the one
  decision it actually is. Noticing the order-dependency is a stop
  signal, not a caveat to ship with.

The repair is in the asking, not in new law: both malformations on
record were covered by rules the asker already held, so adding a
third rule to catch what two rules caught would be the wrong repair. Worked
instance:
`../../../devlog/2026-08-29-000-The-sweep-sheds-the-asking-law.md`.

The notation already keeps this verdict's slot: a question dissolved
this way is `verdict: dissolved` -- a verdict on the framing, never
on what was asserted.
