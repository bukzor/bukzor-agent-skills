# adjudication.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment about how rulings are taken anywhere in this repo -- a
rule whose reversal would change how any surface or register asks,
settles, or files the owner's word. New law enters at
`standing: agent` and waits on presentation; the collection's own
graduation claim governs its growth.

## What does NOT belong here

The court-to-sigil alignment -> `../../strata.claims.kb/fleet.kb/`.
The record algebra -> `../../strata.claims.kb/standing.kb/`. One
skill's asking or review procedure -> that skill's body, or its own
design ledger. The historical record of a ruling -> `../../../adr/`;
the ADR holds the act, this collection holds the standing. The
operator's personal asking law -> the homedir bank; it outranks this
register and is never restated here.
