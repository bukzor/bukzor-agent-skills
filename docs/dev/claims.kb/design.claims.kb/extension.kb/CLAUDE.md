# extension.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment about how a skill here is built on another -- what it
declares as its basis, what it may add, what it must leave standing.

Open questions live here too, as claims with `standing: open`, and a
question with candidate answers takes the Decision Point shape
(`$ITEM.md` poses it, `$ITEM.kb/` holds one file per candidate --
`Skill(llm-kb)`). Rule one by editing its `standing:`; when it
closes, rewrite `$ITEM.md` as the answer and keep `$ITEM.kb/` as the
record of roads not taken.

## What does NOT belong here

The successor skill's own design commitments -> its own
`design.claims.kb/` once it exists. The historical record of a
ruling -> `../../adr/`.
