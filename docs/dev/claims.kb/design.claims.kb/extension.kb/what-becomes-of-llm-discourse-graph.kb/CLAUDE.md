# what-becomes-of-llm-discourse-graph.kb -- maintenance guide

Candidate answers to `../what-becomes-of-llm-discourse-graph.md`, one
per file. Not a general collection: nothing belongs here that is not
a live option for that one question.

When the question closes, rewrite the `$ITEM.md` as the answer and
keep this directory as the record of roads not taken -- the losers
keep their bodies unedited, since their worth is why they lost.
