# purpose -- why /deformalize exists, and the job it does

`/deformalize` receives a formal account -- normally `/formalize`'s
output -- and reifies it into something the account's owner can read and
poke with a stick. The reification is a probe, not a port, and the job
it does at the seam is fixed: add a glossary and plain-English successor
theories to a formal account that stays formal upstream.
