# Reification Is a Probe

Reification is a probe, not a port. The value is what the reification
breaks in the source, not the faithfulness of the copy.

A formal account can read as internally consistent while sliding over a
seam that only surfaces when something concrete is built from it. That
surfacing is the primary finding of a `/deformalize` run, whatever form
the reification takes -- a plain-English restatement that turns out not
to say what the source claims is exactly as much of a finding as a test
that fails.
