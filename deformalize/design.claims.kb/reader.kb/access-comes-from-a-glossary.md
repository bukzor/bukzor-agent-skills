# Access Comes from a Glossary

Plain-English access comes from a glossary interposed between a
very-formal theory and a very-intuitive one. The formal theory stays
formal.

The declined alternative is to require every theory to be stated in
informal terms. That works directly against `/formalize`'s posture and
its deliverable: the formal vocabulary is what makes a claim pin
anything down, and an account rewritten to avoid it pins down
proportionally less. Interposing costs one theory and keeps both ends at
full strength.
