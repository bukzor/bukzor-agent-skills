# reader.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment about who can read the delivered account and in what
vocabulary, about the glossary that carries formal terms across to plain
words, or about how the output forms `/deformalize` can produce rank
against each other.

## What does NOT belong here

`/deformalize`'s own step-by-step procedure (the review step, the
trash/-to-promotion lifecycle) -- those are directives, and belong in
`../../SKILL.md` until or unless a commitment inside them is contested.
