# A Glossary Is a Theory of Claims

A glossary is a set of claims taking the very-formal theory as prior,
each defining a deformalized alternative to one of its terms. The
informal theory then takes the glossary as prior, so the chain runs
formal -> glossary -> informal.

Four things follow, and they are why this shape was chosen over a
side-table of term pairs:

- writing the glossary is a step that can run while the formal theory
  exists and the informal one does not yet;
- the informal theory may be ruled to depend on the glossary, so it
  never reaches past it to the formal terms;
- the formal theory sits at the core of the poset and the informal one
  at a leaf, which is the right way round -- the leaf is what gets
  rewritten;
- several deformalized theories can share one smaller set of
  glossaries.
