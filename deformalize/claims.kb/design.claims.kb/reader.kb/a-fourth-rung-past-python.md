# A Fourth Rung, Past Python

`DEFORM_ORDER` ranks three forms and stops at Python. The question that
motivated this whole ladder, historically, was sharper than "readable
code": *"if i wanted a formal system to help me retain the important
properties when i have a 'good idea' and change the system, how would
I? Does agda have any edge over Lean... or dark-horse formal systems?"*
A Python witness answers "does this law hold on an instance"; it does
not answer "is this law machine-checked in general," which is what a
claim that actually needs certainty, not just a passing test, requires.

Unbuilt, and not yet claimed anywhere else in the ladder
(`Skill(llm-claims)`'s own `PURPOSE` names typed kernels and
proof-transport systems as harder rungs of the same ladder in the
abstract, but `/deformalize` itself has no path to one). Future work:
this stays out of `SKILL.md` until it is real -- a fourth rung named in
the manual before it exists is a promise the skill can't keep.
