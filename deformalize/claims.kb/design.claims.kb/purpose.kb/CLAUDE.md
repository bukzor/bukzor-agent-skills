# purpose.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

Why `/deformalize` exists, what makes its output valuable, and the job
it does at the seam with `/formalize`.

## What does NOT belong here

- How the job gets discharged -> `../reader.kb/`, which holds the
  glossary shape and the ranking among output forms.
- Vocabulary of the notation itself -> stays out entirely; a claim
  needing it belongs in `Skill(llm-claims)`'s or `Skill(llm-claims-kb)`'s
  own design, not here.
