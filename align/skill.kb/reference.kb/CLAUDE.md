# reference.kb -- maintenance guide

Docs a trigger in `../must-read.kb/` points at for the method behind
its directive.

## What belongs here

- A method or lens the skill applies, one file per subject.

## What does NOT belong here

- The directive and its occasion -> `../must-read.kb/`.
- Why the method is the way it is -> an ADR in `../../../docs/dev/adr/`.

## When to add

When a trigger's directive needs a method too long to state in the
trigger.
