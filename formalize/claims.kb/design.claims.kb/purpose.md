# purpose -- what the formalization is for

`/formalize` puts an agent into formal-theory-design-expert posture over
a body of informal design work, and boils that design down to its
fundamental units: claims, and the structures relating them. That basis
is bought for six uses, and the value of every other commitment in this
ledger is what it contributes to them.

The basis pays immediately -- open questions clarify and tied decisions
break as soon as it is on hand -- and it pays later, because a structure
that pins the design down keeps a property settled across a change
instead of surrendering it to be re-derived by hand.
