# Plain English Belongs to /deformalize

The plain-English burden belongs to `/deformalize`
(`../../../../deformalize/claims.kb/design.claims.kb/purpose.kb/deformalize-adds-glossary-and-successors.md`).
`/formalize` stays formal.

This is what makes readability and the formal-theory-design-expert
posture compatible. Held by one skill, they pull against each other at
every step and the readable phrasing wins, because it is always
available and always feels like an improvement. Split across two
skills, each runs at full strength and the seam is the glossary
`/deformalize` owns.
