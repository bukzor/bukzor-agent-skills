# Other People Are a Door Left Open

Other people using the skill is a distant concern, and a door left open.

The order of those two clauses is the ruling. A choice that serves the
owner and closes the door to other users is still made for the owner;
where two choices serve the owner equally, the one that keeps the door
open wins. Nothing is built today for a second user.
