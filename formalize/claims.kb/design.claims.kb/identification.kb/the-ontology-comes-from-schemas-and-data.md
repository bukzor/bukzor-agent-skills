# The Ontology Comes from Schemas and Data

The ontology comes from the schemas and the data; the prose is read for
motivation.

Prose says what the author meant to build. Schemas and data say what
they built, and a formalization of the intent will pin down a system
nobody is running. Where the two disagree, the disagreement is a finding
worth reporting -- and the ontology still follows the data.
