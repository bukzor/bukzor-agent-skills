# identification.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A requirement on a single identification, or on a single claim: what it
must state, what evidence counts, what its refutation is worth, what it
records about its own staleness.

## What does NOT belong here

- The order identifications are produced in, and how many -> `../run.kb/`.
  This collection judges one identification held in the hand; the
  procedure decides how many hands go looking.
- Whose vocabulary the finished account is written in -> `../reader.kb/`.
