# The Deliverable Is the Basis

What a run delivers is the design boiled down to its fundamental units:
claims, and the structures relating them.

Two things this rules out, both of which a run will drift toward if
left alone:

- a survey of the design, which restates it at the same grain;
- a naming of mathematics the design resembles, which adds vocabulary
  and subtracts nothing.

The test is subtraction. A basis is smaller than what it accounts for.
