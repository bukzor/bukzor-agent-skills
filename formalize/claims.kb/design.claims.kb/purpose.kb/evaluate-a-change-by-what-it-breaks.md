# Evaluate a Change by What It Breaks

The basis is bought as a rigorous medium for discussing a proposed
change, and the discussion has a definite shape: the evaluation of a
change is which standing claims it breaks.

That shape is what makes the medium rigorous. It replaces a debate about
whether a change is good with an enumeration of what it costs, and it
makes the answer checkable by anyone holding the basis. It is the same
operation as CONSIST run against a design that does not exist yet.
