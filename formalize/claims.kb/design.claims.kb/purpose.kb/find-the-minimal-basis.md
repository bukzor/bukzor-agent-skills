# Find the Minimal Basis

The basis is bought to refine the design: find the minimal possible
basis, and eliminate the complexity that minimality shows to be
unnecessary.

Minimality is the operative word. A basis that merely reorganizes the
design leaves every part of it standing; a basis driven to minimal
exhibits the parts that no other part needs, and those are the parts to
cut. The design shrinks because the basis did.
