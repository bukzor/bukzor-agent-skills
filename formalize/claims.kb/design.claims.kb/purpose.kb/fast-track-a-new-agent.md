# Fast-Track a New Agent

The basis is bought to bring a new agent to deep understanding of the
design quickly.

An agent that reads the basis holds the design's commitments and their
support structure in a fraction of the tokens the design itself costs,
and arrives able to argue about it. This is the use most sensitive to
`reader.kb/` -- an account a reader has to decode first has spent the
saving it was bought for.
