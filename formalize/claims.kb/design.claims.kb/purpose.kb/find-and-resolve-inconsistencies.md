# Find and Resolve Inconsistencies

The basis is bought, first, to find inconsistencies in the design and
resolve them.

Two claims of one design can each read well in isolation and contradict
each other once both are stated in the same vocabulary. Confining claims
to a stipulated ontology is what forces that meeting, so the collision
becomes visible at the moment of filing.
