# The Unbuilt Uses Narrow the Design Space

AUTOCHECK and EXTRACT usefully narrow today's design space; keeping them
in view informs today's choices.

They inform at the strength of a heuristic. A choice that would foreclose
mechanization is worth a second look, and a choice that happens to serve
it is worth a small premium -- and that is the whole of their authority
today. Treating them as hard requirements would buy machinery for uses
nobody has yet paid for.
