# Formal-Theory-Design-Expert Mode

`/formalize` is a mode before it is a procedure: it puts the agent into
the posture of an expert in formal theory design, working over a body of
informal design work.

The mode is the load-bearing part. Every step in `run.kb/` is a way of
holding that posture under pressure, and a run that follows the steps
from outside the posture produces a document shaped like a formalization
with none of its value.
