# design.claims.kb -- maintenance guide

The design of `/formalize`, kept as a claim ledger in the notation the
skill produces. `../design.claims.md` is the reader's entry point and
carries the poset.

## What belongs here

A commitment the skill makes, or a goal it answers to -- anything whose
standing could be contested and whose reversal would change the
procedure or the bar.

## What does NOT belong here

Instructions for running `/formalize` -> `../SKILL.md`. Those are
directives: nothing about them is contestable in the way a claim is.
The tell is the standing field -- if the honest value is `user` for every
file in a collection, the field carries no information and the
collection is a manual.

Commitments about `/deformalize`'s own procedure -> that skill's ledger.
What lives here is only the seam: which burden `/formalize` hands off,
and what shape the handoff takes.
