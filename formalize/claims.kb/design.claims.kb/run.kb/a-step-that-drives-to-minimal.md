# A Step That Drives to Minimal

The procedure carries a step that drives the basis toward minimal.

The operation is elimination: for each claim, ask what still stands if
it goes. A claim the others entail is a consequence, and belongs
recorded as one; a claim nothing else needs is a candidate for cutting,
along with the part of the design it was accounting for.

This is the step that turns MINIMAL from a hope into an output. Left
implicit, a run stops at *a* basis, and a basis that merely reorganizes
the design has cut nothing from it.
