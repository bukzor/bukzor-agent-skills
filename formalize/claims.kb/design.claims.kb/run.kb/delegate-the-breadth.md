# Delegate the Breadth

Delegating breadth -- subagents surveying a large corpus -- helps a lot
with TOKENS.

Breadth is the delegable part because its product is a report, and a
report is small. Judgment is the part that stays home: which structures
to conjecture, what killed each, and where the itches were, all depend
on holding the whole picture at once.
