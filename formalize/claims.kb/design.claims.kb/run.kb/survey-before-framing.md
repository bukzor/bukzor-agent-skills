# Survey Before Framing

A run surveys the material wide before framing anything, and reports
three things before it theorizes: the recurring shapes, the tensions,
and the places that itch.

Reporting them first is what keeps the survey honest. An agent that has
already picked a frame reads the material for confirmation, and the
itches -- the parts that resist saying cleanly -- are exactly what a
frame chosen early smooths over. They are also the best conjecture
seeds a run gets.
