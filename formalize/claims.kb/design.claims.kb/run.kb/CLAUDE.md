# run.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A step of the procedure, an ordering constraint between steps, or a
standing pressure a run works under (spend, simplicity, unification).
This is the collection `SKILL.md` mirrors -- editing one without the
other leaves the manual asserting what the design has stopped
committing to.

## What does NOT belong here

- The bar a single identification clears -> `../identification.kb/`.
- The use a step pays for -> `../purpose.kb/`. A step cites its use;
  restating the use here duplicates it into two places that drift.
