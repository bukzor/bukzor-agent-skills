# A Step That Hunts Inconsistencies

The procedure carries a step that hunts inconsistencies, and it runs
over the claims a run has just produced.

CONSIST is a headline use, and for a long time the procedure had no step
that produced it -- inconsistency-finding was left to fall out of
filing. It does fall out, sometimes, which is exactly why the gap was
easy to miss.

The hunt has a mechanical starting point: each claim's staleness
condition, evaluated against the data and against the other claims. What
it finds is adjudicated in place, since a contradiction discovered and
smoothed over is the one finding the run was most worth paying for.
