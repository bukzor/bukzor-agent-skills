# Look for a Simpler Formalism

A run periodically reviews its formalism and attempts to find a simpler
one of equal value.

Periodically, because the good simplification is usually invisible at
the moment of first framing and obvious two steps later, once the
structure has been used on real claims. Equal value is the bar that
keeps this from becoming dilution: a simpler formalism that pins down
less is a worse one.
