# Sort the Open Questions

A run names the open questions the design faces, then sorts them into
three piles: the ones the formalism decides, the ones it dissolves, and
the ones that stay open.

This is where FREE gets collected. The dissolved pile is the surprising
one and the most valuable: a question that stops being askable once the
vocabulary is fixed was a confusion, and its disappearance is a result
to report by name.

The declined form was stating each question twice, once as experienced
and once as well-posed. The content survives in the three piles; the
ritual of the double statement does not.
