# Truth Is Level-Relative

Designs have layers. A claim's truth is relative to the level it is
stated at, and where an upper level is definable from the level below,
saying so is load-bearing.

Two claims of one design can look contradictory purely by being stated
at different levels, so locating a claim on its level is part of stating
it. Definability across levels is the payoff: an upper level definable
from a lower one inherits everything the lower one pins down, which is
PINS applied to the stack rather than to a single structure.
