# An Identification Cashes Out

An identification cashes out as five things, each stated in place:

- what the things are;
- what operations act on them;
- what must hold of those operations;
- one smallest example, drawn from the actual data;
- the condition under which it goes stale.

An identification carrying fewer than five is a name with an argument
attached to it, and names transfer nothing. The declined alternative is
the citation: pointing at a literature where the structure is developed
proves the structure exists somewhere, and leaves the question of
whether it holds *here* exactly where it was.

The smallest example is the part that most often kills a candidate, and
it is cheap, which is why it belongs in the bar rather than in a review.
