# identification -- what it takes to count as one

An identification is the unit `/formalize` produces and the unit it can
get wrong. This theory sets the bar: what an identification must carry,
where its ontology comes from, what a refutation ships as, and the
condition every claim records against its own future.

The bar is the same whether the structure is famous or invented. Names
carry no weight here; the five parts do.
