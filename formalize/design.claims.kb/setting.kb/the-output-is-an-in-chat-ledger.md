# The Output Is an In-Chat Ledger

The output is the in-chat `Skill(llm-claims)` ledger format, and that
format lands the account at an appropriate size on its own.

The size property is why no page budget is imposed. One claim per line,
each self-contained, gives a length that tracks how much the design
actually has to say. A one-page target on top of it would be a second,
arbitrary constraint, and the arbitrary one wins whenever they disagree.
