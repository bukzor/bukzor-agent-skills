# The Basis Settles Questions for Free

Boiling a design down to its basis clarifies open questions and breaks
tied decisions for free, as soon as the basis is on hand.

This is an observed effect, and it is the reason the skill exists. It
also sets the honest expectation for a run: the payoff arrives at the
moment of having the basis, so a run that produces a basis and stops has
already delivered. Elaboration past that point is bought separately.
