# purpose.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A use the basis is bought for, or a property of the basis that explains
why those uses are reachable at all. Audit this collection when the
question is whether `/formalize` still solves the problem it was built
for.

## What does NOT belong here

- How a use gets served -> `../identification.kb/` for the bar a
  formalization must clear, `../run.kb/` for the step that pays for it.
- Vocabulary of the notation -- label, sigil, arrow, ledger. A use
  stated in those terms has smuggled a delivery decision into the
  premises, and will approve of whatever the skill already does. Those
  words live in `../setting.kb/`, where they can be thrown away.
