# A Structure Earns Its Place by What It Pins Down

A structure earns its place by how much of the design it **pins down**:
what it entails stays settled across a change, and what it leaves loose
must be re-decided by hand.

This is the criterion that ranks candidate structures against each
other, and it is why entailment is the relation worth chasing. A
structure that is true of the design but entails nothing about it has
described the design and bought nothing; a structure that entails a
dozen of the design's properties has bought a dozen re-derivations.

The declined criterion is elegance. A structure is judged here by
transfer, and transfer is countable.
