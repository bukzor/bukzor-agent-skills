# Check a Proposed Change Automatically

The basis is eventually meant to evaluate a proposed change for
consistency with the design automatically, with no agent in the loop.

Unbuilt. It is recorded as a commitment because it is the mechanized
form of DISCUSS, and because holding it in view constrains what a claim
may look like today: a claim whose breaking condition lives only in an
agent's reading of the prose can never be checked by a program.
