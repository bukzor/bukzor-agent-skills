# Deadline Escalation

A `before:` + time condition is a constraint, not a deferral: the
task must complete before the instant, so attention should increase
as it approaches — the inverse of deferral's suppress-until
(`../040-design.kb/task-deferral.md`).

Today: deadlines live in prose or in the operator's head.

Satisficed when: a deadline-bearing task surfaces with increasing
insistence as its instant nears. That it escalates rather than fires
once is the contract; the escalation's shape and schedule are
instance data.
