# Pre-Share Sweep

Design-next's multi-operator entry names the one irreversible event:
sharing a store that one-operator habits filled with personal
content. Its trigger is "about to be shared for the first time" — a
condition an agent can notice during planning, whose payload demands
a sweep-shaped response (find operator-private content, settle
identity conventions) rather than a plain read.

Today: a `trigger:` field nothing evaluates, guarding an
irreversible act.

Satisficed when: the condition is expressible so the floor
guarantees at least planning-time noticing, and the surfaced body
can direct a judgment sweep before the share proceeds.
