# Occurrence-Counted Conditions

Some conditions are stated as counts: design-next's structured-query
trigger is "third occurrence of an orientation question `ls`/`grep`
cannot answer cleanly." As written, no sweep — mechanical or
judgment — can evaluate it: the occurrences are tallied nowhere.

Today: such conditions are unobservable; their triggers can only
fire by accident of memory.

Satisficed when: the evaluability rule
(`../040-design.kb/trigger-desc.md`) forces the choice at authoring
time — restate the condition over observable state, record the tally
somewhere the desc can name, or mark it judgment-only with eyes
open.
