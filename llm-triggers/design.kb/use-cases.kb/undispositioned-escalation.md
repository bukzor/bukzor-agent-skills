# Undispositioned-Wake Escalation

A surfaced wake the agent ignores, session after session, must not
stabilize as noise: repetition without consequence teaches agents
and operators to stop reading surfaced items — the attention-side
analog of the untrusted store.

Today: nothing surfaces, so nothing escalates.

Satisficed when: the sweep can distinguish first surfacing from
repeated surfacing, and a repeatedly-undispositioned wake can
escalate — say, from an agent-visible line to an operator-facing
report. Thresholds are instance data; that escalation exists is the
contract.

Sketch, non-normative: escalation may itself be just a wake
condition over the sweep's own emissions — "surfaced N times without
disposition" — rather than dedicated machinery.
