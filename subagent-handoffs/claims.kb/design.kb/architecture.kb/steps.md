# Steps are the unit's checkpoint

An agent keeps its Steps as checkboxes, marking each `[~]` on starting
it and `[x]` on finishing it. With its staged edits, that shows a
resuming agent where its unit stopped: the `[~]` step is the one in
flight, and everything before it is done.
