# Mission

A coordinator drives a multi-unit implementation through sub-agents,
and no lost context, crash, or mid-course change of requirements costs
more than the unit in flight.

The people with the problem are a user who wants a plan built larger
than one agent's context holds, and the coordinator acting for them.
Done in one session, the work outgrows the context; done by sub-agents
briefed in chat, every brief and reply is lost with the session that
held it, and nobody can review what an agent was actually asked.

This rung is the root: nothing above it motivates it.
