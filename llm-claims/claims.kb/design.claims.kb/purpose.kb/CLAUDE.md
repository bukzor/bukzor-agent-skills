# purpose.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A goal, and the constraint it places on the design. This is the
collection to audit when the question is whether the notation still
solves the problem it was built for, rather than whether it is
internally consistent.

## What does NOT belong here

- How a goal gets met -> `../good-smells.kb/` for the criterion,
  `../notation.kb/` for the decision.
- Vocabulary of the notation itself — label, sigil, arrow. A goal stated
  in terms of sigils has smuggled a design decision into the premises,
  and will approve of whatever the design already does.
