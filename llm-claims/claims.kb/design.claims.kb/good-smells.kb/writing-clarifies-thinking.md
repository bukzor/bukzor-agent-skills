# Writing Clarifies Thinking

Composing an entry forces the judgment it records. The notation earns
this smell when the writer must resolve an internal question to finish
typing the line — an open-vs-signed sigil can't be written without
deciding which is honest. The act of notation is itself the epistemic
checkpoint.

Complements no-forced-early-decisions.md: the notation elicits every
judgment the writer can make now, while providing an honest out (`?`)
for the ones they can't.
