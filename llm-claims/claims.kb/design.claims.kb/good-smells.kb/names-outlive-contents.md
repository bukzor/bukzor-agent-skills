# Names Outlive Contents

A claim's names, its label and its file name, say what the claim is
about, and stay true under every amendment of the body. A claim can
reverse polarity under revision and keep its names, so every existing
reference stays valid and revision is ergonomic: supersede in place,
same label, new content. A label that names the question ("RR: route
requirement") survives any answer; a file named for its locus
(`what-voids-repayment.md`) survives any count of routes.

Two ways of failing it, both from real ledgers:

- a label that encodes the answer ("NC: obligations Name their Check")
  dies with its first revision, and the strike shows it;
- a name that encodes the body's current shape (a label reading "the
  five properties", a file `three-things-void-repayment.md`) dies with
  its next amendment, and nothing shows it: the body changes, the name
  stands, and the name is now false. Both were renamed for their locus
  on 2026-09-16, in kb-dynamics' ledger.

The second is the quieter failure, since amendment strikes nothing.
