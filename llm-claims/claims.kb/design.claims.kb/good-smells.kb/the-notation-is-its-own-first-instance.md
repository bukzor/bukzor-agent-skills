# The Notation Is Its Own First Instance

This skill's commitments are recorded as claims, in this notation —
one file each, here in `design.claims.kb/`, sorted into theories the
way the notation says to sort them. That is the cheapest test
available: a notation that cannot carry its own design will not carry
the user's, and here the failure surfaces during authoring rather
than after adoption.

Two consequences:

- Where the self-application lacks a feature the design commits to,
  one of the two is wrong — a design claim nobody could be bothered
  to apply is a claim to re-examine, not an aspiration.
- A term appearing at two fidelities — a chat line and a file, a
  label and a filename, a sigil and a frontmatter field — is aligned,
  not colliding. Divergence between them is the smell, and renaming
  to dodge the overlap hides exactly the correspondence worth
  checking.
