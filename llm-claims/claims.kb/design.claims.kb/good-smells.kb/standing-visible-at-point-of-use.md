# Standing Visible at Point of Use

A claim's epistemic standing is readable wherever the claim is
referenced, not only where it is defined. A derivation line that shows
the warrant-mix of its premises (`XY <- AB! CD?`) is its own audit: the
reader knows what the conclusion rests on without leaving the line, and
weak foundations are conspicuous exactly where weight is placed on
them.
