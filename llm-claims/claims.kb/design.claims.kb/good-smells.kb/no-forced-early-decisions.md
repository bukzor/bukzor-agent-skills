# No Forced Early Decisions

Entries are writable in order of thought, not in order of structure.
Nothing in the format demands a decision before the writer has made it:
lines are unordered and reorderable, premises can be added on revision,
statuses can be omitted and supplied later. The notation fails this
smell wherever finishing a line requires information the writer doesn't
have yet.

Complements writing-clarifies-thinking.md: elicit every judgment
available now; demand none that isn't.
