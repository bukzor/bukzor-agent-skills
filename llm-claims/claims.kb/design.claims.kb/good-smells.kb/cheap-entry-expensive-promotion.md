# Cheap Entry, Expensive Promotion

Recording a claim is priced as close to the speed of thought as the
medium allows -- zero is impossible, so minimizing is the design
intent, not a guarantee -- and the act of recording claims nothing;
warrant is charged only at status transitions, where the certificate
must exist anyway. Any rule that taxes the act of writing-down —
mandatory fields, required discharge routes, entry-time proof —
suppresses recording, and unrecorded debt is worse than undischarged
debt.
