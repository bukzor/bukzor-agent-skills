# good-smells.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A property by which one notation can be judged better than another,
with a concrete example of failing it. A criterion nothing could fail is
not a criterion.

This notation's own labels and sigils may appear as *illustrations* —
they are not in the ontology, and a criterion that cannot be stated
without them is really a decision (`../notation.kb/`).

## What does NOT belong here

- The decision a criterion justifies -> `../notation.kb/`.
- Rules for operating a ledger -> `../../skill.kb/`. Those run the
  notation; these grade it.

## Standing

`open` is the honest default: most of these were extracted from a single
design conversation and have never been tested against a second
notation. `user` means the user adjudicated it, not that it reads
convincingly.
