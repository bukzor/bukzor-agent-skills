# Every Render Is a Patch

Restating a claim supersedes it; a claim not mentioned stands as it was.
So a reply need not repeat the whole ledger to update part of it — list
what changed and stop.

Two things this settles, not just permits: silence endorses the last
version, so a claim that no longer holds must be restated or struck,
never just dropped from the next render; and a partial render never
means "the ledger is now only these" — `claim list` and flush are the
two points where completeness itself is the point, and only there is
the whole ledger the right answer.

Declined: requiring a full render on every reply, on the theory that a
diff a reader has to reconstruct by diffing two full lists themselves is
less legible than one that names its own delta. It also prices every
small correction at the size of the whole ledger, which is what made the
policy worth stating rather than assuming.
