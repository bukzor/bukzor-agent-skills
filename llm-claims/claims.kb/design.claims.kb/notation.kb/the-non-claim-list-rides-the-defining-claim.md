# The Non-Claim List Rides the Defining Claim

The enumeration NON_CLAIM_TOKENS calls for is a frontmatter field,
`non-claim-tokens:`, on a defining claim -- beside `ontology:`, as its
dual: ontology names the words a theory owns, this names the tokens
its prose disclaims.

- **Placement:** the defining claim, because that is where a theory
  already makes lexical declarations, and the list then travels with
  the ledger through every rendering.
- **Precedence:** reachability wins. The list is consulted only for a
  token that does not resolve, so a listing can never silence a live
  citation.
- **Scope:** the declaring theory's own interior, at minimum. Whether
  it reaches further is deliberately unstated: the owner doubts the
  ruled ontology cascade
  (`../ownership.kb/containment-admits-without-an-arrow.md`), that
  review is queued in `llm-claims/.claude/todo.md`, and this field
  takes no wider position until it rules.

The field name and the precedence rule are agent-picked from the
owner's candidates and constraints; veto by editing this file.
