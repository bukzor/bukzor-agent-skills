# The Sigil Signs the Judge

A sigil records whose judgment a claim rests on -- `!` the user's, `+`
the agent's, `?` no one's yet -- and bare means no judge is needed: a
checkable fact, or a claim that follows from its premises.

This supersedes the earlier reading, where bare meant "asserted,
believed sound" and `!` meant "warranted, by fiat or by check." That
reading kept a hidden oracle: an unsigned assertion carried someone's
judgment without saying whose. Under this one the four marks exhaust
the space -- no judge needed, user, agent, no judge yet -- so no fifth
sigil is possible; and a certified claim goes bare with its check
named (`-- certified(CHECK)`), since the check made it a fact and
re-running it is the appeal.

Grounds: this is the fixed point of the notation's own rules --
name the judge, no marks on derivations, authority belongs to acts --
and a second instance of the system arrived at it independently
(prototype.llm-stet, 2026-08-08), where the user ruled it in.
