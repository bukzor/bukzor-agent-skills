# Two Scalar Fields, Judge Separated From Verdict

The reconciliation brief's original proposal:
`verdict: open|accepted|rejected|retracted|dissolved` plus
`judge: agent|user|<path>`. Same expressiveness as TERSE at a wider,
flatter enum -- still one assessor, still a verdict that must be one
of a fixed small set. Superseded within the same session once
`the-sigil-signs-the-judge.md` turned out to already give `standing:`
this reading, so the split bought nothing a cheaper edit didn't.
Recorded because the option was seriously proposed and argued
through, not to vanish from the record for having lost.
