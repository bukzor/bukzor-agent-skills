# ownership.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment of the licensing law -- who may say a stipulated name,
what finding follows when they may not, what the law itself makes of
a repair, and the procedure that adjudicates what the law leaves
undefined -- stated with what it cost and what it bought.

## What does NOT belong here

- Per-name fleet adjudications (this sibling double, that idle
  import) -> the scans report them; a ruling lands where the word is
  stipulated, not as a claim about the law.
- The audit procedure a law implies -> `../../skill.kb/` and
  llm-claims-kb's `skill.kb/self-audit.kb/`.
- The runnable law -> `../ownership.py`, the witness the `verify:`
  lines name.
