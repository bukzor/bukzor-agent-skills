# A dangling label is charged at home

The label sort's finding is a self-charged unresolved citation: a
mention that resolves to nothing in its own namespace plus imports is
charged to the namespace containing it, and label answerability is
that namespace's own -- no other ledger is answerable for the dead
citation or entitled to prosecute it, even the ledger that defines
the label elsewhere. This is the second parameter separating the
sorts: a trespass is prosecuted by an owner, a dangle is confessed by
its holder.
