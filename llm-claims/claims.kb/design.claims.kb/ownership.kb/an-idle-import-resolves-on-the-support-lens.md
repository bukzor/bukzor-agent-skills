# An idle import resolves on the support lens

Reading an idle import, the question is support alone -- the
vocabulary half of the fused `why:` is already known idle, and
only the support half can save the arrow:

- **no claim of the taker rests on the cited theory**: the arrow
  asserts support that does not exist. Cull it -- checking first
  for stranded words, since an import is also a license and
  cutting one can mint a trespass elsewhere -- or, where the
  support really lives in a different theory, retarget the arrow
  there.
- **the support is real but misfiled**: what the taker rests on is
  a duplicate of a ruling homed elsewhere, or the matter it cites
  is a lodger the prior's own text rules off-topic. Refile -- dedup
  toward the ruling's home, extract the lodger to its own claim --
  and the arrow dissolves without being ruled on. The tell is
  mutual reach: the prior's prose referring back into the taker.
  A file-path cite is a reference, backticked or not; only the
  scan's exemptions are narrower than that, not the reading's.
- **the support is real and at home**: keep the import. The
  vocabulary went unsaid to the scan, not to the reader -- the trace
  is there, in prose naming the prior's claim labels -- and
  WHY_IS_FUSED's factoring smell is repaired by saying a word.

The strongest keep-tell -- interior claims citing the cited
theory's claims in their own `why:` -- is machine-readable, and the
scan reads it: those imports never queue. What reaches the reader
is support riding the arrow alone, a presumption the defining claim
makes of the prior with no interior echo -- or no support at all.

A cited theory that stipulates no words makes every import of it
idle by definition -- an artifact of the scan, which its output
says, not a defect in the arrow. Worked 2026-08-20: of the first
queue, three keeps and one retarget, the retarget being the fleet's
live stranding case; the citation tell then went scan-read, and the
last entry resolved as a refiling -- the taker's one dependent
sentence duplicated the migration plan's escrow ruling, and the
prior's citing section was itself a restatement of a strata claim,
extracted to a one-line application beside its consumers.

Amended 2026-09-03 under WHY_IS_FUSED's sharpening: the third

denies. A keep must be able to point at the trace, and until the scan
