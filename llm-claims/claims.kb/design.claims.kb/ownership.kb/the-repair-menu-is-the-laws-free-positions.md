# The repair menu is the law's free positions

A finding is one relation over four movable parts, so the repair
menu is read off the law rather than invented -- one repair per
position the repairer may change:

- **cull** -- the owner's side: the word was no coinage, strike it
  from the ontology;
- **move** -- the speaker's position: the claim is misfiled,
  refile it inside the owner;
- **admit** -- the license: import the owner's theory;
- **uniquify** -- the word itself: rename at a stipulator, so the
  two no longer collide.

The caveat the verify line carries: the witness demonstrates that
each repair discharges a finding, which shows the menu sufficient
for its fixture, not exhaustive in principle -- the enumeration is
asserted here, not derived there. It is the one step of the law the
witness enumerates rather than earns.
