# Zero force empties the docket, not the question

A stipulation at zero force puts nothing on the repair docket: no
finding, no repair to choose. That is all it settles. Whether the
word *should* be stipulated is a different question that force
cannot see -- an unowned word scores zero by construction, so
screening by force would wave through every word nobody has claimed
yet, which is exactly the population SHOULD_OWN? asks about.
