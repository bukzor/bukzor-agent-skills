# Sibling doubles contend for one word

Two theories in one ledger stipulating one word are two owners, and
that is an error charged to both stipulations: at least one of them
has to lose the word. Contention is a state of the stipulations, not
of the occurrences -- occurrences of a contended word are simply
undecidable until it clears, and the scan skips them rather than
guessing.

The law prescribes no repair, because the repair menu edits one
position at a time and there is no position here that is wrong on its
own. A person picks the loser; the scan's whole job is to keep the
pair visible until someone does.

The declined reading is that the law is *undefined* there -- true of
the licensing function, and useless, because it describes the
formalism's gap rather than the corpus's fault and names nobody to
answer for it.
