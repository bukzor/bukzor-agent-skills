# How Should a Mention Without Support Surface?

WHY_IS_FUSED's second direction -- a theory names a claim or word of
a prior and rests on none of it -- is the one the fleet cannot see.
Where no import licenses the mention, the trespass and citation scans
already report it. Where one does, the vocabulary lens fires on the
mention itself, the import reads healthy by construction, and nothing
goes on to ask whether support followed.

The obstacle is that "rests on none of it" has no mechanical witness.
Absence of an arrow is not one, since the arrow under audit is the
import. Measured 2026-09-07, the nearest proxy -- an import whose only
witness is vocabulary, with no interior `why:` landing in the prior --
fires on eighteen of the seventy arrows that land on a theory at all,
and it fires on the obviously sound: the
ownership theory says five of the notation theory's words thirty-one
times and would be queued. Under the same ruling that motivates the
queue, that lexical trace *is* the witness, so the proxy contradicts
its own warrant.

What the measurement does isolate is a thin tail -- three imports
whose entire witness is one word said once: `protocol` reaching `view`
for *view*, `record` reaching `view` for *cache*, `good-smells`
reaching `purpose` for *adoption*.

Three answers, and the real choice is what a standing queue is worth:

- **Read the tail once, build nothing.** Three entries is a sitting,
  not a tool, and the corpus regenerates them slowly. Recommended: it
  is the only answer that adds no instrument, and an instrument whose
  precision is unknown costs more than the findings it earns.
- **Rank the witness and expose the thin end.** A `--thin` queue over
  the same machinery, ordered by how few words carry the import,
  with the threshold as a flag. Cheap, and it survives the sitting --
  but it names no principled cut, so the threshold is the arbitrary
  constant this design keeps declining elsewhere.
- **Ship the proxy as it stands.** Eighteen entries, mostly healthy.
  Named to be argued against: a queue whose typical entry is sound
  teaches the reader to skim it, which is how a queue dies.

Two of the four repairs the ruling lists already have scans: narrowing
the prior's ontology is what `--candidates` proposes, and the
unlicensed mention is what `--trespass` reports. So a new queue's
marginal catch is the licensed mention alone, which is the smallest of
the four.
