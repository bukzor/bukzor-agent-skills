# Should exposition avoid coinages?

Should synthesis prose avoid stipulated coinages entirely, on the
ground that its goal is exposition and a coinage is a tax on the
reader who was not there for the stipulation?

The ownership stake is gone: REACH licenses an ancestor because it is
not a sibling, structurally and unconditionally, so a synthesis
saying its parts' words is no longer resting on a move a yes here
would knock out. What survives is a writing-quality question with no
consequence for the law, and it stays open on those terms.
