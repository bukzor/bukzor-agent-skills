# ownership -- who may say a stipulated name

One law licenses every stipulated name in a ledger: a reference is
licensed by the absence of an owner or by the speaker sitting in the
owner's reach. Words (`ontology:` stipulations) and labels (claim
names) are the law's two sorts; they differ in what an unowned name
means and in the shape of the finding, never in the law.

Theories section; the label sort states the design behind
`Skill(llm-claims-kb)`'s mentions machinery -- the notation's own
file form, not a second skill's commitments. `ownership.py` beside
this file is the runnable witness the `verify:` lines name; the
