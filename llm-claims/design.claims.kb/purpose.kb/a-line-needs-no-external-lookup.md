# A Line Needs No External Lookup

The ledger is worth more the less it must look elsewhere. A line
needing an external lookup before it can be acted on has not finished
being written.

That makes under-statement the characteristic failure, not verbosity —
and it fails silently: the line reads fine to its author, who still
remembers, and is worthless to everyone after.
