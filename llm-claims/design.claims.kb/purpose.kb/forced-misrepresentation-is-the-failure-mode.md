# Forced Misrepresentation Is the Failure Mode

"It's irrational if we're forced into a representation that indicates
something other than what's true." The general law
`every-claim-sound-open-or-retracted.md` states for one case (a
judged-negative claim with nowhere to record "no" reads as accepted
-- "the fourth state is not a gap in the record but a false record"):
a format that cannot hold what is actually so does not fail by
omission, it fails by lying, because the reader has no way to tell
"unrepresentable" from "not the case." A missing field is silent
about itself; a wrong-shaped field is not.
