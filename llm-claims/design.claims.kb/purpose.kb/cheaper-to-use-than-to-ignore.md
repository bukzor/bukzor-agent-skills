# Cheaper to Use Than to Ignore

The ledger competes with keeping no ledger, not with a better
notation. It is adopted only where recording a claim, referring to
one, and governing the set each cost less than the confusion they
prevent — and the comparison is made line by line, in the middle of
doing something else, by someone who did not set out to keep a ledger
today.

This is the goal the economies serve:

- free entry, with warrant charged at the transitions
  (../good-smells.kb/cheap-entry-expensive-promotion.md);
- governance in one line over labels
  (../good-smells.kb/operations-fit-in-a-breath.md);
- no glyph a chat client mangles
  (../good-smells.kb/survives-dumb-media-and-tools.md).

It also settles feature arguments: any proposal must answer who pays
for it on every line, not merely who benefits on the lines that use
it.
