# Pending speech sorts by candidate owner

While a double waits for adjudication, a third party's saying of
the contested word sorts by evaluating TRESPASS under each
candidate resolution:

- licensed under every candidate: safe now, whatever is ruled;
- licensed under none: a finding now, and it need not wait for the
  docket;
- licensed under some: turns on the ruling, and waits with the
  docket.

The partition is computable from the law as it stands, so it is
scan work rather than reading work; the scan does not compute it
yet, which is a work item, not a gap in the law.
