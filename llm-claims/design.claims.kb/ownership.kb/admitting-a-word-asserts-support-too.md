# Admitting a word asserts support too

Admit is the cheapest repair on the menu -- one line in `why:` --
and it is not free: under the fused `why:` an import asserts support
as well as vocabulary, so an admit taken for vocabulary alone
manufactures exactly the lens disagreement WHY_IS_FUSED calls a
factoring smell. Take it only where "my theory rests on theirs" is
true in both senses; otherwise the honest repair is one of the other
three.
