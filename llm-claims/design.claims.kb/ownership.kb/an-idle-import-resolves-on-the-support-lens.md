# An idle import resolves on the support lens

Reading an idle import, the question is support alone -- the
vocabulary half of the fused `why:` is already known idle, and
only the support half can save the arrow:

- **no claim of the taker rests on the cited theory**: the arrow
  asserts support that does not exist. Cull it -- checking first
  for stranded words, since an import is also a license and
  cutting one can mint a trespass elsewhere -- or, where the
  support really lives in a different theory, retarget the arrow
  there.
- **the support is real**: keep the import; the unsaid vocabulary
  is WHY_IS_FUSED's factoring smell, repaired by saying a word or
  by nothing at all.

The strongest keep-tell -- interior claims citing the cited
theory's claims in their own `why:` -- is machine-readable, and the
scan reads it: those imports never queue. What reaches the reader
is support riding the arrow alone, a presumption the defining claim
makes of the prior with no interior echo -- or no support at all.

A cited theory that stipulates no words makes every import of it
idle by definition -- an artifact of the scan, which its output
says, not a defect in the arrow. Worked 2026-08-20: three keeps and
one retarget cleared the first queue, the retarget being the
fleet's live stranding case; with the citation tell scan-read, one
entry remains, kept by reading -- its taker presumes the cited
theory's reform ruling without saying or citing anything of it.
