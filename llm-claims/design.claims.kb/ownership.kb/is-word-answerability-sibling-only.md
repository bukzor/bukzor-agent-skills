# Is word answerability sibling-only?

For words, is the owner answerable only between siblings? What rides
on the answer is the residue the ancestor ruling left: a cousin or
nephew saying an owned word is today unlicensed and unanswerable --
permanently silent, a violation no one may prosecute. A smaller hole
than the container case THREE_MOVES dissolved, but the same shape.
