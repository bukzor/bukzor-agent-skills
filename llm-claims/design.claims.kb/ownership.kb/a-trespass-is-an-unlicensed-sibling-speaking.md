# A trespass is an unlicensed sibling speaking

The word sort's finding takes two conjuncts: the reference is
unlicensed, and the site actually says the word. The finding names its
own prosecutor, because REACH bars exactly the positions the owner may
charge -- so "unlicensed" and "answerable" are one conjunct, not two.

Speech is the conjunct that keeps the docket honest: an ontology entry
another theory could violate but none does costs nothing to leave
standing, and a scan reporting it would be reporting a hypothetical.
For labels the finding takes a different shape entirely, charged at
the mention site with no prosecutor needed (DANGLING).
