# Label namespaces are per-theory

Word exclusion is ledger-local -- another ledger is out of
jurisdiction, and two ledgers stipulating one word coexist legally.
A label lives in a per-theory namespace, imported by a defining
claim's `why:`; ledgers are disjoint namespace-universes by default.
The sole fleet-wide fact about either sort is recognition -- whether
a name is a label at all -- which is why the mentions scan reads
every ledger while charging findings only at home.

The declined alternative, one fleet-global label namespace, conflated
recognition with jurisdiction: the scan does read the fleet, but only
to know a name is a label somewhere before flagging it here.
