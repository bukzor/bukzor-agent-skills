# Sibling doubles leave the law undefined

Two siblings stipulating one word leave ownership with no value at
all: the licensing law is undefined there, not merely violated. Each
excludes the other from a word it claims, so the law prescribes no
repair -- the position is adjudicated by a person, not computed by a
scan, and the scan's whole job is to keep the position visible until
someone does.
