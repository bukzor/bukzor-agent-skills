# Should exposition avoid coinages?

Should synthesis prose avoid stipulated coinages entirely, on the
ground that its goal is exposition and a coinage is a tax on the
reader who was not there for the stipulation? A yes would moot the
ancestor move exactly where THREE_MOVES leans on it -- the synthesis
would not be saying the words at all -- and reopens that ruling,
which is tentative by its own marking.
