# What decides a word should be owned?

Given that force triages findings and cannot rank the unowned
(SCREENING), what does decide that a word wants a stipulation? The
notation's own rule -- coinages only, words the theory fixes a
meaning for that a reader could not have brought -- names the class
but not a procedure for a contested case. The corpus holds 310
stipulations today (observed 2026-08-20, after the plain-English
adjudication culled 16). One procedure is now on the record: the
user's two-move menu for a plain-English double -- remove the
ownership, or expand the entry to the term of art actually coined,
narrowing the claim (2026-08-20; 'product' became 'synchronized
product' this way).
