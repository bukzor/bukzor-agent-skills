# A trespass needs license, answerability, and speech

The word sort's finding takes three conjuncts: the reference is
unlicensed, the owner is answerable at the speaker's position, and
the site actually says the word. Drop the middle conjunct and every
distant use becomes prosecutable by a stranger; keep it and the
finding names its prosecutor. For words the owner is answerable
between siblings (how far else is ANSWERABLE_IS_LOCAL?); for labels
answerability collapses into the namespace itself (DANGLING).
