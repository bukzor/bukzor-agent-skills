# A synthesis citing its children is broken

A synthesis that cites bunches of its own nested theories in `why:`
is broken: containment already carries that relation, and the
ancestor move makes the edges needless for vocabulary too. The
arrows worth keeping are the ones whose collapse would move the
synthesis -- which its own children rarely are, since they are what
it is made of.
