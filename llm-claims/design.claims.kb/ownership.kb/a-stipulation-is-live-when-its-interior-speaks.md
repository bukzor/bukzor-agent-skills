# A stipulation is live when its interior speaks

A stipulation is live when some site in its own interior says the
word, and idle otherwise. Liveness is the owner's side of the
ledger, where force is the neighbours': a stipulation can be idle
and heavy, or live and weightless, and the two audits never
substitute for each other. The idle imports the queue collects are
this claim read across an arrow: an import whose taker never says
the taken words.
