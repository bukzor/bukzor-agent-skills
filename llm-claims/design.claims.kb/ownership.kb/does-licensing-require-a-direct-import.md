# Does licensing require a direct import?

Does the import move of reach require a direct `why:` arrow, or does
an import carry its own imports onward? The shipped mentions rule
says onward -- "a defining claim's `why:`, transitively" -- but the
measured traffic is zero: of 92 fleet-label citations, 91 resolve in
their own ledger, 1 through a direct import, none through the chain
alone (probe of 2026-08-20). So the cutout currently licenses
nothing, and eliminating it would cost no existing claim.

The word sort wears the same question as the tred-dropped `prior:`
edges decision in `llm-claims/.claude/todo.md`: an edge carrying
vocabulary is load-bearing however the drawing thins it, and an edge
carrying nothing is the idle-import queue's business.
