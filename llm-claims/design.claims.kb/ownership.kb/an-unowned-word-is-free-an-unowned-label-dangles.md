# An unowned word is free; an unowned label dangles

An unowned word is free: no stipulation, no owner, no finding,
whoever says it. An unowned label is dangling: a citation with
nothing behind it, and the defect exists precisely because no owner
does. This is the first of the two parameters separating the sorts.
