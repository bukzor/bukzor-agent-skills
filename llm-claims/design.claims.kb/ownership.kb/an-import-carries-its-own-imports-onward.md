# An import carries its own imports onward

The import that readmits a sibling licenses through a chain, not only
through a direct arrow. A taker has to be able to read what it rests
on, and a prior's claims are written in that prior's own priors'
words, so a reach truncated at one hop leaves a taker unable to read
its own premises.

The two sorts settle it together. The label sort has always resolved
transitively -- the shipped mentions rule reads a name through the
theories a claim's own imports import -- and the sorts differ in what
an unowned name means and in the shape of the finding, never in the
law. A scan answering one sort's version of a question one way and the
other's the other way is the disagreement OWNERSHIP is stale when it
finds.

What the truncated reading cost was measurable: of 123 word findings,
104 were sites reaching the owner through a chain -- every one a
theory reading its own premises, none a smear (probe of 2026-08-20).
The declined alternative is that truncation, whose appeal is that it
keeps a deep taker from inheriting a vocabulary it never named. That
discipline is real but belongs to the author, not the checker: a
theory states each law in the weakest vocabulary that supports it, and
the idle-import queue is what asks whether an arrow earns its keep. A
mechanical cutoff at one hop buys the discipline nowhere and costs the
ability to read a premise.
