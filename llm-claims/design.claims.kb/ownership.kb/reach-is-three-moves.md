# Reach is three moves

An owner's reach licenses three regions: its interior, because
containment admits downward without an arrow; its ancestors, because
a synthesis naming its parts' words is better than admissible; and
the interiors of the theories that import it. Cousins and nephews
remain outside.

The ancestor move is the tentative part. It dissolves an anomaly --
before it, a container saying its nested theory's word was unlicensed
yet unanswerable, a violation with no possible prosecutor -- but
EXPOSITION? names the counterargument that would firm or reverse it,
and DIRECT_ONLY? asks whether the import move tolerates a chain of
arrows or requires one.
