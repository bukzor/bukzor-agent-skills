# purpose -- what the notation is for

The notation exists to hold one invariant -- every claim sound, open, or
retracted, for both parties -- at a cost low enough that a chat adopts it
instead of keeping nothing. What it is measured against is that absence,
not a stricter system: typed kernels and proof transport are further
rungs of the same ladder, and this rung is the one a conversation can
reach.
