# notation.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A decision that could have gone the other way, stated with what it cost
and what it bought. A commitment nobody could have disagreed with is a
description of the notation, not a claim about it — and belongs in
`../../SKILL.md`, where descriptions are the point.

## What does NOT belong here

- The criterion a decision answers to -> `../good-smells.kb/`. Cite it;
  do not restate it, or the criterion acquires two versions that drift.
- The instruction that follows from a decision -> `../../SKILL.kb/`. A
  reader there wants to know what to do; the reasoning is a tax on them
  and the point for you.
