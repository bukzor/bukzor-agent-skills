# Cycles Are Unproven

`AB <- CD` and `CD <- AB` satisfy "proven to the extent its premises
are" both as all-sound and as all-open. Take the least fixpoint: a ring
stands only on what enters it from outside, so a closed ring of mutual
support is `?`, however confident it reads.

This also keeps standing cheap to compute — provenness stays an
inductive predicate over the claim graph rather than a search for a
consistent assignment.
