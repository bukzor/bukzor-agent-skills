# A Claim Earns Nothing by Restating Its Premises

A claim that follows from its premises needs no separate statement —
that is `bare`'s whole job (`the-sigil-signs-the-judge.md`). Writing it
out anyway as a signed claim is redundant on its own terms, and
self-defeatingly so: a rule against redundant claims that is itself
stated as a claim, rather than left to follow from `bare`, violates
itself the moment it is written down.

Caught reflexively, not in the abstract: a proposed rule this shape
("assert nothing that already follows from what's required") was
rejected on exactly this ground — asserting it would already be the
violation it names.
