# stance.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A commitment that would still hold if this notation were thrown away and
redesigned from scratch — about justification, authorship, or what earns
a distinction. Three or four claims is the expected size; a stance that
needs a dozen is doing some other theory's work.

## What does NOT belong here

Anything mentioning a label, a sigil, a file, or a reader. None of those
words are in this theory's ontology, and a claim needing them belongs
downstream — `../good-smells.kb/` if it judges a notation,
`../notation.kb/` if it decides one.
