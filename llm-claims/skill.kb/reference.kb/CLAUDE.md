# reference.kb -- maintenance guide

Vocabulary a reader needs that `SKILL.md` links directly rather than
carries.

## What belongs here

- Reader-facing vocabulary too long for `SKILL.md`, one subject per
  file. `../CLAUDE.md` says why a second file here wants justifying.

## What does NOT belong here

- Operations and writing disciplines -> `../must-read.kb/`.
- Why a rule is the way it is -> `../../claims.kb/design.claims.kb/`.

## When to add

Rarely: only when a reader cannot decode a ledger line without it and it
does not fit `SKILL.md`.
