# Before Writing an Arrow

That `<-` is motivation rather than entailment is in `SKILL.md`; this is
what follows for the writer.

Point arrows at the claims whose collapse would make you revisit this one
-- not at everything that was on your mind at the time. That is the test
that keeps them useful: retraction sweeps by following them
(`../after/retracting-a-claim.md`), and a sweep over decorative arrows is
noise.
