# reference.kb -- maintenance guide

Docs a consumer loads on demand (`REFERENCE_KB`, in the fleet ledger's
`authorship.kb/`). Today: decision lenses, which judge the decision a design
claim records rather than walk the record, so they are not quality passes.
`../must-read.kb/when/deciding-what-a-design-claim-should-say.md` reaches
them.

## What does NOT belong here

- A walk over a design record that finds a fault -> `../quality-passes.kb/`.
- A directive bound to an occasion -> `../must-read.kb/`.
