# Deferred Work Needs No Collection

`070-future-work.kb/` dissolves. A deferred idea is a claim filed
under the rung it concerns, in one of three states the notation
already distinguishes:

- nobody has decided -- `standing: open`, its text naming what would
  settle it;
- decided and unbuilt -- `todo:`, the body stating the future state;
- decided against -- struck, with `verdict:` and a ground.

The bucket conflated all three, and filing by tense rather than by
subject put an idea about the mission at maximum distance from the
mission. Scheduling the work remains `Skill(llm-subtask)`'s job, which
is the warrant `skill://llm-claims/claims.kb/design.claims.kb/notation.kb/an-open-question-needs-no-new-mark.md`
already established for the ledger generally.
