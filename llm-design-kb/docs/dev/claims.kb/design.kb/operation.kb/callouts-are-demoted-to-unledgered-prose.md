# Callouts Are Demoted to Unledgered Prose

`> [!TODO]` and `> [!QUESTION]` stay legal only in documents outside
the ledger -- READMEs, contracts, synthesis files -- where there is no
frontmatter to carry standing. Inside a record they are superseded:
`[!QUESTION]` is `standing: open`, `[!TODO]` is `todo:`.

One word now spans all three surfaces -- the callout's `[!TODO]`, the
chat line's `(todo)`, and the file's `todo: true` -- which is what
makes the demotion a translation rather than a loss.

A callout that churns is a promotion signal: the document is
accumulating claims that want a judge and a ground, and the repair is
to ledger-ize them rather than to keep editing blockquotes.
