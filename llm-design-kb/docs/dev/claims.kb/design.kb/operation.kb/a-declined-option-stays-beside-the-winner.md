# A Declined Option Stays Beside the Winner

A rejected alternative is a struck claim carrying `verdict:` and its
ground, filed next to the claim that beat it. At the **second
contending answer** the question takes `Skill(llm-kb)`'s
decision-point shape: `$ITEM.md` poses the question, `$ITEM.kb/` holds
one file per candidate, chosen and declined alike; when it closes,
`$ITEM.md` is rewritten as the answer and the collection stays as the
record of roads not taken.

This obviates the incumbent's three-shape "Alternatives Considered"
ladder (inline "why not X", parallel list, sub-kb), which was the same
growth curve without the standing. What the ledger adds is a judge and
a ground on each declined option -- the two things that stop the next
session from re-proposing it and re-paying the whole argument.

It also revises the incumbent's stance that alternatives are "a
footnote on the design space, not a historical log". The forward-facing
half is right and kept: the record states what the system is. But a
declined option with a verdict is not history, it is a live boundary
of the design space, and demoting it to a footnote is what made
re-litigation cheap.
